> The net profit ratios are calculated as reported net profit divided by revenue, rounded to one decimal place. Xiaomi's figure uses reported profit for the year, not adjusted net profit.[1][2][3]

> Apple's FY2024 reported net income included a one-time US$10.2 billion tax charge, so its year-on-year profit growth is affected by that comparison base.[1]

> Smartphone market shares are IDC preliminary estimates of global unit shipments, not shares by market value. The 2024 comparison uses the 2024 figures shown in IDC's 2025 table.[4]

> Apple reports on a fiscal year ending in September. McDonald's and Xiaomi report on calendar years ending in December. Apple and McDonald's figures are in US dollars; Xiaomi figures are in RMB.[1][2][3]

> Customer service spending is not shown separately in these reports. Broad SG&amp;A or operating expenses include other costs, so they should not be labelled as customer service expenditure.[1][2][3]
