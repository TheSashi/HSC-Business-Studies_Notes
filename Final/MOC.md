# Business Studies — Final Notes

> **Exam-ready** revision material for HSC Business Studies. Each topic note follows the syllabus structure: **Role → Influences → Processes → Strategies**, and ends with worked examples, a confusing-terms glossary and a definitions table. For raw research, see the Brain vault.

## Topics

### 1. [[Operations]]
Role and strategic role (cost leadership, differentiation), influences, transformation processes and the 4 Vs, technology and layout, monitoring and control, **sequencing and scheduling with a worked critical path analysis**, performance objectives, supply chain, outsourcing, **inventory management vs valuation with worked FIFO/LIFO**, quality management, resistance to change, global factors.

### 2. [[Marketing]]
Role and approaches, influences on customer choice, ethical and legal influences, the marketing process, SWOT and product life cycle, market research, objectives and target markets, the 4 Ps and extended 3 Ps, e-marketing, global marketing strategies, and Section IV essay structures.

### 3. [[Finance_Management]]
Role and the five objectives, sources of finance (internal, debt, equity), financial institutions, government and global influences, planning and implementing, **the three financial statements**, the **seven ratios with worked calculations**, limitations of financial reports, ethical issues, and the four strategy families including **global financial management and hedging**.

### 4. [[Human_Resources]]
Role, outsourcing and stakeholders, influences, the legal framework, **the HR cycle (acquisition → development → maintenance → separation)**, HR strategies, motivation theories, dispute resolution, organisational structures, and the **effectiveness indicators of HRM**.

### 5. [[Case_Studies]]
Apple Inc., McDonald's Corporation, Perfect Fresh Pty Ltd. Financial data, analysis and model answers.

## Quick Formulas — Finance

> Full worked examples in [[Finance_Management]] §3.5.

```
LIQUIDITY
  Current Ratio        = Current Assets / Current Liabilities        ideal 2:1

SOLVENCY
  Gearing Ratio        = Total Liabilities / Owners' Equity          ideal 0.5:1 to 1:1

PROFITABILITY
  Gross Profit Ratio   = (Gross Profit / Sales) x 100                higher is better
  Net Profit Ratio     = (Net Profit / Sales) x 100                  higher is better
  Return on Owners'    = (Net Profit / Owners' Equity) x 100         higher is better
    Equity

EFFICIENCY
  Expense Ratio        = (Total Expenses / Sales) x 100              lowest possible
  Accounts Receivable  = Step 1: Sales / Accounts Receivable
    Turnover             Step 2: 365 / Step 1 answer                 ideal 30 days

BREAK-EVEN
  Break-Even (units)   = Fixed Costs / (Selling Price - Variable Cost)
  Contribution         = Selling Price - Variable Cost per unit
  Margin of Safety     = Actual sales - Break-even sales
```

## The Three Financial Statements

```
CASH FLOW STATEMENT
  Opening Cash Balance + Cash Receipts - Cash Payments = Closing Cash Balance

INCOME STATEMENT
  Sales
  Less COGS  (Opening Stock + Purchases - Closing Stock)
  = Gross Profit
  Less Expenses
  = Net Profit

BALANCE SHEET
  Owners' Equity = Assets (Current + Non-Current) - Liabilities (Current + Non-Current)
```

## The Verbs

| Verb | What to do | Marks guide |
|---|---|---|
| **Identify / State** | Name it — no explanation | 1–2 |
| **Outline** | Sketch in general terms | 2–3 |
| **Describe** | Features and characteristics | 2–3 |
| **Distinguish** | Differences between two things | 3–4 |
| **Explain** | Cause and effect — how and why | 3–4 |
| **Analyse** | Relationships and implications | 4–6 |
| **Discuss** | For and against, then judgement | 5–6 |
| **Assess / Evaluate** | Weighted judgement with evidence | 7–8 |
| **Recommend** | Course of action with justification | 7–8 |

## Exam Tips

1. **Always apply theory to a case study** — Apple, McDonald's, BYD.
2. **Use specific data** — ratios, figures, percentages, dates.
3. **Show interdependence** between the four functions.
4. **Interpret, don't just calculate** — a ratio means nothing without a judgement on it.
5. **Link back to the strategic role** — cost leadership/differentiation (Ops), profit maximisation (Marketing), the five objectives (Finance), effectiveness indicators (HR).
6. **Know your verbs** and match answer length to marks.
