## Topics

### 1. [[Operations]]
Operations management, quality, technology, lean production, supply chain, capacity planning.

### 2. [[Marketing]]
Marketing process, 4Ps/7Ps, segmentation, targeting, positioning, digital marketing, consumer law.

### 3. [[Finance_Management]]
Financial ratios (profitability, liquidity, gearing, efficiency), cash flow, working capital, budgets, break-even, sources of finance.

### 4. [[Human_Resources]]
Recruitment, training, performance management, motivation theories (Maslow, Herzberg, McGregor), organisational structures, employee relations.

### 5. [[Case_Studies]]
Apple Inc., McDonald's Corporation, Perfect Fresh Pty Ltd. Financial data, analysis, model answers.

## Quick Formulas

```
Current Ratio = CA / CL (ideal 2:1)
Gearing = TL / OE (ideal 1:1)
GPM = GP / Revenue × 100
NPM = NP / Revenue × 100
ROE = NP after tax / OE × 100
AR Turnover = 365 / (Revenue / AR) (ideal 30 days)
Break-Even (units) = FC / (SP - VC)
```

## Exam Tips

1. Always apply theory to a case study
2. Use specific data — ratios, figures, dates
3. Show interdependence between functions
4. Know your verbs: identify (1-2 marks), explain (3-4 marks), evaluate (7-8 marks)
