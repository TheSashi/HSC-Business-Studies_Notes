# Past HSC Operations Extended Response Questions

> Sashank's practise responses to past HSC operations questions (2015-2019). Apple case study throughout.

---

## How to Write Extended Response Questions

**Structure:**
- **Introduction** - Write your main point to answer the extended response question, be specific about what part of the syllabus you are using, do not be general, introduce the case study/ies that you are going to use in your essay.
- **Body Paragraphs** - integrate the case study to assist you in answering the question. Use separate paragraphs for each main point that you are stating. A minimum of 2 body paragraphs but recommended to do 3 paragraphs.
- **Conclusion** - 1-2 sentences summarising each body paragraph.

## 2015: Evaluate the importance of operations strategies in achieving competitive advantage.

**Evaluate** - make a judgement based on criteria - the criteria for this question is achieving competitive advantage.

**Choose 3 Operation Strategies** (Performance Objectives/Overcoming resistance to change NOT recommended):
- SCM - Choose between Logistics OR Global Sourcing OR E-Commerce
- Technology
- Outsourcing
- Inventory Management
- Quality Improvement - Choose between Quality Control OR Quality Assurance OR Quality Improvement
- New Product Design and Development
- Global Factors - Choose between Global Sourcing OR Economies of Scale OR Scanning and Learning OR Research and Development

### Sample Introduction

It is very important for Apple to choose the most effective operational strategies to assist them in achieving competitive advantage. As Apple produces new technology such as smartphones, tablets and computers, they are in a very competitive market therefore it is extremely important that they choose the most appropriate operational strategies to ensure that they are achieving cost leadership and product differentiation to maintain their competitive advantage. This essay will demonstrate the importance of Apple using Global Sourcing, Quality Control and Research and Development to maintain their competitive advantage.

### Sample Body Paragraph (Global Sourcing)

**Judgement sentence** - It is extremely important for Apple to use Global Sourcing to maintain their competitive advantage.

**Definition** - Global sourcing is the sourcing of raw materials from businesses overseas.

**Cause** - Apple sources their raw materials from over 30 different countries from Asia, Europe and America to produce their iPhones.

**How** - They have a very complex supply chain as they have contracts with suppliers that are able to supply their raw materials for a competitive price and still maintain their high quality standards as expected by the customers.

**Effect** - Even though this causes some issue with logistics in ensuring that Apple can coordinate all of the raw materials to be sent to their outsourcing partners in China and India it does save them money. This assists them in achieving cost leadership while maintaining quality. This leads them to maintaining a competitive advantage over their main competitors as demonstrated by the increase in market share to 17.7% in 2024.

**What does this mean for the business?** - By minimising costs and increasing market share this assists Apple in maintaining a competitive advantage and achieving their overall goal of profit maximisation.

## 2016: Evaluate the response of operations management to external influences

**Evaluate** - make a judgement based on criteria - the criteria for this question is responding to external influences.

**Choose 3 Operation Strategies to link to 3 influences:**
- SCM - link to technology, quality expectations, cost based competition
- Technology - link to technology, quality expectations, cost based competition
- Outsourcing - link to cost based competition, CSR
- Quality Management - link to quality expectations
- Global Factors - link to globalisation

### Essay

Operations management plays a crucial role in enabling a business to respond effectively to external influences in order to remain competitive. External influences such as technological change, quality expectations, and globalisation can significantly impact how a business operates. This essay will evaluate the response of Apple's operations management to external influences by examining the operations strategies of Technology, Quality Control, and Global Sourcing. These strategies demonstrate how Apple successfully responds to external pressures while maintaining competitive advantage in the global technology market.

It is extremely important for Apple's operations management to respond to the external influence of technological change through the use of technology as an operations strategy. Technological change refers to rapid advancements in digital systems, automation, and innovation that affect how products are produced. Apple responds by heavily integrating advanced technology such as automation, robotics, and AI into its manufacturing and product design processes. This allows Apple to produce high quality products efficiently while reducing human error and production costs allowing them to become a cost leader. As technology continues to evolve rapidly, Apple's ability to adapt its operations ensures faster production times and innovative products that meet consumer expectations. This response is highly effective as it allows Apple to remain competitive in a cost based and innovation driven market, demonstrating strong operations management.

Apple's use of quality control is a vital operations strategy in responding to the external influence of rising quality expectations. Quality expectations refer to consumers demanding reliable, durable, and high performing products. Apple applies strict quality control measures at every stage of production, including testing components, inspecting finished products, and monitoring supplier standards. This ensures that defective products are minimised and brand reputation is maintained. Although quality control increases production costs, Apple is able to justify this through pricing and strong brand loyalty and reputation. This response is effective as it allows Apple to meet customer expectations and differentiate its products from competitors, reinforcing its competitive advantage.

Globalisation has significantly influenced Apple's operations, requiring a strategic response through global sourcing. Globalisation refers to businesses operating on an international scale, with access to overseas markets, suppliers, and labour. Apple sources raw materials and components from multiple countries such as China, Taiwan, and South Korea to reduce costs and access specialised suppliers. This allows Apple to benefit from lower production costs while maintaining high quality standards. However, global sourcing also creates challenges such as supply chain disruptions and ethical concerns. Despite this, Apple's operations management effectively manages these risks through long term supplier contracts and strict compliance standards. Overall, this response is effective as it enables Apple to remain competitive in a global market while responding to the pressures of globalisation.

In conclusion, Apple's operations management responds effectively to external influences through the use of technology to address technological change, quality control to meet rising quality expectations, and global sourcing to respond to globalisation.

## 2017: Analyse the effect of globalisation on operations management

**Analyse** - show the relationship between and draw out implications - show the relationship between globalisation and operations management.

### Essay

Performance objectives and operations strategies are closely linked, as operations strategies are implemented to achieve specific performance objectives such as cost, quality, speed, dependability and flexibility. Performance objectives guide how a business designs and manages its operations in order to remain competitive. This essay will analyse the relationship between performance objectives and operations strategies by examining Apple as a case study. Specifically, it will analyse how Apple uses Technology, Quality Control, and Global Sourcing as operations strategies to achieve its performance objectives and maintain competitive advantage in the global technology market.

There is a strong relationship between technology as an operations strategy and the performance objectives of cost and speed at Apple. Technology refers to the use of advanced machinery, automation and digital systems in production. Apple uses highly automated manufacturing processes and advanced software systems to increase production speed and reduce reliance on labour. This allows Apple to produce large volumes of products efficiently and quickly meet global demand when new products are released. By reducing production time and lowering labour costs, Apple is able to improve speed while also controlling costs. This relationship shows that technology directly supports Apple's performance objectives by enabling faster production and improved cost efficiency.

Apple's use of quality control demonstrates a clear relationship between operations strategies and the performance objectives of quality and cost. Quality control involves inspecting products during and after production to ensure they meet required standards. Apple conducts strict testing of components and finished products to minimise defects and ensure high reliability. This allows Apple to meet customer expectations for premium quality, strengthening its brand reputation. Although quality control can increase costs due to testing and inspections, it reduces long term costs by minimising returns, repairs and warranty claims. This relationship highlights how quality control supports both quality and cost performance objectives.

There is a strong relationship between global sourcing and the performance objectives of cost and dependability at Apple. Global sourcing involves obtaining raw materials and components from overseas suppliers. Apple sources components from countries such as China, Taiwan and South Korea, allowing access to specialised suppliers at lower costs. This reduces production costs while ensuring a consistent supply of high-quality components. By forming long term contracts with reliable suppliers, Apple improves dependability by ensuring materials are delivered on time and production schedules are maintained. This relationship shows how global sourcing helps Apple achieve lower costs while maintaining dependable operations.

In conclusion, there is a clear relationship between performance objectives and operations strategies at Apple. Technology supports cost and speed, quality control supports quality and cost, and global sourcing supports cost and dependability. By aligning its operations strategies with its performance objectives, Apple is able to operate efficiently and maintain competitive advantage in a highly competitive global market.
