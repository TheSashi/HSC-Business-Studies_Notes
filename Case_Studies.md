# Case Studies — HSC Business Studies

## How to Use Case Studies in the HSC

The HSC exam requires you to **apply theory to real businesses**. Every extended response and most short answer questions need case study references.

**Case study framework:**
1. **Background** — industry, size, location, products/services
2. **Operations** — how they produce/deliver, strategies used
3. **Marketing** — target market, marketing mix, positioning
4. **Finance** — performance (ratios), funding, budgets
5. **HR** — staffing, training, culture
6. **Recommendations** — based on analysis, justified with theory

## Apple Inc. — Finance Case Study

### Background
- **Industry:** Consumer electronics, software, services
- **Size:** One of the world's largest companies by market cap
- **Products:** iPhone, Mac, iPad, Wearables, Services (Apple Music, iCloud, App Store)
- **Global presence:** Americas, Europe, Greater China, Japan, Rest of Asia Pacific

### Financial Data (2024-2025)

### Financial Ratios (Apple)

| Ratio | 2025 | 2024 | Trend |
|-------|------|------|-------|
| **Current Ratio** | 147,957/165,631 = **0.89:1** | 152,987/176,392 = **0.87:1** | Slightly improved but below ideal 2:1 |
| **Net Profit Margin** | 112,010/416,161 × 100 = **26.9%** | 93,736/391,035 × 100 = **24.0%** | Improved — more revenue retained as profit |
| **Gearing (D/E)** | 285,508/73,733 = **3.87:1** | 308,030/56,950 = **5.41:1** | Improved — less reliance on debt |
| **Accounts Receivable Turnover** | 365/(416,161/39,777) = **34.9 days** | 365/(391,035/33,410) = **31.2 days** | Slightly worsened — slower collection |

### Key Takeaways for HSC Answers

**Strengths:**
- Strong profitability improvement (NPM 24% → 26.9%)
- Revenue growth across all segments
- Services revenue growing fastest (+13.5%)
- Gearing improved significantly (reduced debt reliance)
- Low inventory levels (efficient inventory management)

**Weaknesses:**
- Current ratio well below ideal 2:1 (0.89:1) — relies on fast inventory turnover and AR collection
- AR turnover slightly worsened (31 → 35 days)
- Heavy reliance on iPhone (50% of revenue)
- Exposure to China market risk

## McDonald's Corporation — Finance Case Study

### Background
- **Industry:** Fast food / Quick Service Restaurant (QSR)
- **Size:** Global — 40,000+ restaurants in 100+ countries
- **Business model:** Primarily franchise-based (95% franchised)
- **Products:** Burgers, chicken, fries, beverages, breakfast, desserts

### Financial Ratios (McDonald's Historical)

| Ratio | 2022 | 2021 | 2020 | 2019 | 2018 |
|-------|------|------|------|------|------|
| **Current Ratio** | 1.43:1 | 1.78:1 | 1.01:1 | 0.98:1 | 1.36:1 |
| **Net Profit Margin** | 26.65% | 32.49% | 24.63% | 28.20% | 27.87% |
| **Gross Profit Margin** | 56.97% | 54.17% | 50.77% | 52.32% | 50.96% |
| **Expense Ratio** | 30.32% | 21.68% | 26.14% | 24.12% | 23.09% |

### McDonald's 2022-2023 Analysis (from assessment)

| Ratio | 2022 | 2023 | Analysis |
|-------|------|------|----------|
| **Current Ratio** | 1.43:1 | 1.16:1 | Worsened — increase in short-term borrowing |
| **Gearing (D/E)** | -598% | -869% | Negative due to share buyback; still relies more on debt than equity |
| **Net Profit Margin** | 27% | 33% | Improved — increased sales while maintaining costs |
| **Accounts Receivable Turnover** | 31.74 days | 32.88 days | Slightly worsened but close to ideal 30 days |

### McDonald's Financial Strategy Recommendations

**Example recommendation:** "It is recommended for McDonald's to use cost centres to improve their profitability in the future."

**Why:** Cost centres minimise costs by allowing McDonald's to allocate budgets and responsibilities to each cost centre to find ways to minimise costs such as buying raw materials locally rather than controlling from head office in America.

**Marketing link:** McDonald's implemented a new promotional campaign in 2023 giving away Marvel movie figures with large meals, incentivising more customers and increasing sales from $23,182M to $25,493M.

## Perfect Fresh Pty Ltd — Operations & Marketing Case Study

### Background
- **Industry:** Food manufacturing, wholesale supply
- **Product:** Fresh salads for caterers and wholesale outlets
- **Market:** Sydney region
- **Key innovation:** Plastic storage bag extending salad shelf life

### Problems Identified
1. Late deliveries to customers
2. Poor product presentation (packaging)
3. Low productivity and high staff absenteeism

### Analysis Framework

**Target Market:**
- Sydney-based caterers and wholesale outlets
- Need: freshness, presentation, reliability
- Knowing this market ensures marketing strategies are relevant and operations are aligned

**Operations Process — Inputs (Packaging):**
- Packaging is a critical input directly influencing freshness and visual appeal
- Poor-quality packaging → damage, reduced freshness, poor presentation
- Solution: high-quality, durable, visually appealing packaging

**Operations Process — Transformation (Technology):**
- Modern equipment improves speed, consistency, hygiene
- Produces uniform, professional look
- Without right technology → delays, inconsistent presentation
- Solution: upgrade technology to improve productivity, reduce errors

**Marketing — Product Strategy:**
- Recommendation: Premium, branded packaging strategy
- Creates strong visual identity
- Stands out in wholesale settings
- Reinforces the brand's commitment to quality and freshness
- Attracts and retains customers who value presentation

## Tips for Using Case Studies in the HSC

### Short Answer Questions
- Always name the business
- Link theory directly to the case study
- Use specific data/figures when available
- "For example, Apple's net profit margin improved from 24.0% to 26.9%, demonstrating that their revenue growth strategies have been effective."

### Extended Response / Business Report
- Reference the case study in every section
- Use ratios and financial data to support arguments
- Compare across years or against industry benchmarks
- Make specific, justified recommendations

### Common Australian Case Studies by Topic

| Topic | Business | Why |
|-------|----------|-----|
| **Operations** | Toyota Australia, Atlassian, Cochlear, CSL | Innovation, quality management, global supply chains |
| **Marketing** | Qantas, Woolworths, Canva, Thankyou Group | Strong branding, segmentation, digital marketing |
| **Finance** | Commonwealth Bank, BHP, Apple, McDonald's | Strong financial data, clear ratios |
| **HR** | Google Australia, Salesforce, Westpac | Progressive HR practices, diversity programs |
