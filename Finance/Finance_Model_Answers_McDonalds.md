## McDonald's Ratio Analysis (2022 vs 2023)

### Liquidity — Current Ratio
| Year | Current Ratio |
|------|--------------|
| 2022 | 1.43:1 |
| 2023 | 1.16:1 |

**Comparison:** The current ratio has worsened from 1.43:1 in 2022 to 1.16:1 in 2023. The current ratio measures liquidity — the ability to pay current liabilities with current assets. **Similarity:** Inventory value was around $52 million both years. **Difference:** Huge increase in short-term borrowing of $2 million, which explains why the ratio worsened.

### Solvency — Gearing (Debt to Equity)
| Year | Gearing |
|------|---------|
| 2022 | (598)% |
| 2023 | (869)% |

**Note:** Ratio is negative due to McDonald's share buy-back, but they still rely more on debt than equity.

### Profitability — Net Profit Ratio
| Year | Net Profit Ratio |
|------|-----------------|
| 2022 | 27% |
| 2023 | 33% |

**Comparison (with Yum Brands benchmark: 2022=19%, 2023=23%):** McDonald's NP ratio improved from 27% to 33%. **Similarity:** Total operating costs were steady ($13,811.6M → $13,847M). **Difference:** Revenue increased from $23,182M to $25,493M, explaining the improvement.

### Efficiency — Accounts Receivable Turnover
| Year | Days |
|------|------|
| 2022 | 31.74 days |
| 2023 | 32.88 days |

## Extended Response Scaffold (Part B)

### Introduction/Executive Summary
- Brief introduction about the business (2-3 sentences)
- State what you will cover: specific ratios and profitability analysis
- Be specific with ratios being analysed

### Body Paragraph Structure (Assess)
For each ratio:
1. **Introductory sentence** — state the ratio and its trend
2. **Cause and How** — explain what caused the change
3. **Effect** — what this means for the business
4. **Judgement/Link** — link to financial objectives and long-term goals

**Model Answer:** Expense ratio in 2024 = 21,000/700,000 = 30%. This means for every $1 of sales, $0.30 are expenses. Comparing to industry average of 20%, this travel agency's expense ratio is worse because it is higher than industry average.

## Financial Strategies Scenario — XYZ Retail

**Scenario:** Small retail business with high inventory levels, low cash reserves, and high accounts receivable.

### Sample PEEL Answer (JIT Strategy)

**Point:** One strategy XYZ Retail can use to overcome high inventory levels is the JIT inventory management strategy to control their current asset of inventory.

**Evidence:** XYZ Retail has high inventory levels which is increasing costs as they have to pay to store the inventory and there is a risk it will become obsolete.

**Explanation:** By using JIT, XYZ Retail will reduce storage costs, minimising expenses to improve profitability. It will also reduce the risk of inventory not converting to sales due to obsolescence.

**Link:** By reducing inventory costs, XYZ Retail can reduce expenses and maximise profit which can be reinvested into the business leading to business growth.

**Key insight:** Top students integrate strategies from other topics (operations) and link them back to finance impact.

## Key Exam Points

- Always show **working** for ratio calculations
- **Compare** ratios to previous periods AND industry averages
- Use **relational verbs** (therefore, as a result, consequently)
- **Link to scenario** — always reference the specific business
- **Integrate** knowledge across topics (e.g., operations strategies for finance problems)
