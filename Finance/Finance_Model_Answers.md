## 1. McDonald's Model Answers — Task 1 Assessment

### Current Ratio (Liquidity)

| Year | Ratio |
|------|-------|
| 2022 | 1.43:1 |
| 2023 | 1.16:1 |

**Comparison:** The current ratio has **worsened** from 1.43:1 in 2022 to 1.16:1 in 2023. The current ratio measures liquidity — the ability to pay current liabilities with current assets. **Similarity:** McDonald's inventory was ~$52 million both years. **Difference:** Huge increase in short-term borrowing of $2 million in 2023. Therefore this shows why the current ratio has worsened.

### Gearing Ratio (Solvency)

| Year | Ratio |
|------|-------|
| 2022 | (598)% |
| 2023 | (869)% |

Negative due to McDonald's share buyback — still relies more on debt than equity.

### Net Profit Ratio (Profitability)

| Year | McDonald's | Yum Brands |
|------|-----------|-----------|
| 2022 | 27% | 19% |
| 2023 | 33% | 23% |

**Assessment:** Profitability has **improved** from 27% to 33%. McDonald's implemented a new marketing strategy in 2023 (Marvel promotional campaign with large meals) which incentivised more customers. Revenue increased from $23,182M to $25,493M while operating costs remained steady ($13,811.6M → $13,847M). Therefore profitability strategies have been **effective**.

### Accounts Receivable Turnover (Efficiency)

| Year | Days |
|------|------|
| 2022 | 31.74 |
| 2023 | 32.88 |

### Part B Scaffold — Extended Response Structure

1. **Introduction/Executive Summary** — Brief intro about business, state what ratios will be analysed
2. **Assess each ratio** using scaffold:
   - Introductory sentence (cause and how)
   - Effect on the business
   - Judgement linking sentence
3. **Recommend** ONE strategy to improve financial performance
4. **Conclusion**

### Recommendation Example
"It is recommended for McDonald's to use **cost centres** to improve profitability. Cost centres minimise costs by allowing McDonald's to allocate budgets and responsibilities to each centre to find ways to minimise costs such as buying raw materials locally rather than controlling from the head office in America."

## 2. Past HSC Finance Model Answer (2025)

### Q: Outline the expense ratio for 2024 (2 marks)

**Student Answer:**
"Expense ratio in 2024 = 21,000/700,000 = 30%. This means for every $1 of sales, $0.3 are expenses. Additionally, comparing to industry average of 20% in 2024, this travel agency's expense ratio is worse because it is higher than industry average."

**Markers' Comment:** Achieved 2/2 — showed working, stated meaning, compared to industry average, related to scenario.

### Q: Explain how comparative ratio analysis improves financial outcome (4 marks)

**Student Answer:**
"Comparative ratio analysis can improve this travel agency's financial outcomes. Through comparing the expense ratio in 2024 (30%) to the industry average (20%), because the agency's ratio is higher, it indicates expenses per sales have increased, meaning less net profit. Financial managers can use this data to revise or change financial strategies, leading to better outcomes."

**Markers' Comment:** Achieved 4/4 — linked to scenario, clear cause and effect using relational verbs, provided implications.

### Q: Explain how TWO global market influences could affect solvency (4 marks)

**Student Answer:**
"Interest rates affect solvency because they can cause an increase in current liabilities. When global interest rates increase, the travel agency will have to pay greater interest payments, increasing total liabilities and the debt to equity ratio, resulting in less solvent.

Furthermore, economic outlook impacts solvency. When the outlook is negative, the travel agency will be less likely to take on debt and invest. The debt to equity ratio may decrease or stay the same, maintaining gearing and solvency."

**Markers' Comment:** Achieved 4/4 — linked to scenario, clear cause and effect, provided implications.

## 3. Exam Verb Scaffolds

| Verb | Marks | Scaffold |
|------|-------|----------|
| **Identify** | 1–2 | Name the item directly |
| **Outline** | 2 | Provide characteristics and features |
| **Describe** | 3 | Definition + characteristics/features |
| **Explain** | 3–4 | Define → Cause/Relationship → Effect → Link |
| **Discuss** | 5–6 | Arguments for AND against, balanced |
| **Assess** | 5–6 | Make a judgement based on criteria |
| **Evaluate** | 7–8 | Weigh up options, make justified recommendation |

## 4. Financial Strategies — Scenario Activity

### Scenario: XYZ Retail
Small retail business with wide product range and loyal customer base, struggling with high inventory costs and cash flow problems.

**Recommended Strategies:**
1. **Factoring** — sell accounts receivable for instant cash injection
2. **Inventory management** — reduce stock levels, implement JIT
3. **Distribution of payments** — spread expenses over the year
4. **Discounts for early payment** — encourage faster customer payments

## 5. Comparison Ratio Analysis — Key Points

| Comparison Type | Purpose |
|----------------|---------|
| **Previous Periods** | Track improvement/decline over time |
| **Industry Averages** | Benchmark against competitors |
| **Budget/Target** | Compare actual vs planned performance |

> When writing comparison analysis: always state **what the comparison shows** (improved/worsened), **why** (cause), and **what it means** for the business (implications).
