# Financial Ratios Summary Sheet

> **HSC Business Studies — Finance Topic** | All 7 ratios with formulas, objectives, goals, and linked strategies.

---

## The 7 Financial Ratios

### 1. Current Ratio (Liquidity)

**Shows:** For every $1 of Current Liabilities, how much Current Assets a business has.
**Formula:** Current Assets / Current Liabilities
**Ideal:** 2:1
**Objective:** Liquidity

**Linked Strategies:**
- Control over Current Assets (cash, receivables, inventories)
- Control over Current Liabilities (payables, loans, overdrafts)
- Leasing
- Sale and Leaseback

### 2. Gearing Ratio (Solvency)

**Shows:** For every $1 of equity, how much debt they have.
**Formula:** Total Liabilities / Total Equity
**Ideal:** Between 0.5:1 to 1:1
**Objective:** Solvency

**Linked Strategies:**
- No real strategies — linked to choosing the best source of financing for the business's financial situation

### 3. Gross Profit Ratio (Profitability)

**Shows:** For every $1 of Sales, how much is left over after paying for COGS.
**Formula:** Gross Profit / Sales
**Ideal:** No ideal — compare to previous periods and industry average
**Objective:** Profitability

**Linked Strategies:**
- Revenue Control Strategies — Changing the Marketing Mix
- Cost Controls — Bulk buying, global sourcing, outsourcing, cost centres

### 4. Net Profit Ratio (Profitability)

**Shows:** For every $1 of Sales, how much is left over after paying for expenses.
**Formula:** Net Profit / Sales
**Ideal:** No ideal — compare to previous periods and industry average
**Objective:** Profitability

### 5. Return on Owners Equity (Profitability)

**Shows:** How much return owners get back from their investment.
**Formula:** Net Profit / Total Equity
**Ideal:** No ideal — compare to previous periods and industry average
**Objective:** Profitability

### 6. Expense Ratio (Efficiency)

**Shows:** For every $1 of Sales, how much is spent on expenses.
**Formula:** Expenses / Sales
**Ideal:** No ideal — compare to previous periods and industry average
**Objective:** Efficiency

**Linked Strategies:**
- Cost Controls

### 7. Accounts Receivable Turnover (Efficiency)

**Shows:** How many days it takes for businesses to collect money from customers.
**Formula:**
- Step 1: Sales / Accounts Receivable
- Step 2: 365 / (answer from Step 1)
**Ideal:** 30 days
**Objective:** Efficiency

**Linked Strategies:**
- Factoring
- Discounts for Early Payments
- Credit Policies

## Important Equations to Memorise

### Cash Flow Statement
Opening Cash Balance + Cash Receipts (inflow) - Cash Payments (outflow) = Closing Cash Balance

### Income Statement
Sales
Less: Cost of Goods Sold (Opening Stock + Purchases - Closing Stock)
= Gross Profit
Less: Expenses
= Net Profit

### Balance Sheet
Owners Equity = Assets (Current + Non Current) - Liabilities (Current + Non Current)

## Key Exam Points

- Memorise all 7 ratio formulas and their objectives
- Know the **ideal** ratios (current ratio 2:1, gearing 0.5-1:1, receivables 30 days)
- Always **compare** ratios to previous periods and industry averages
- Link ratios to **strategies** and **financial objectives**
- Use **comparative ratio analysis** to identify trends and issues
