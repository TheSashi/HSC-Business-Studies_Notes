## Key Formulas

```
Current Ratio = Current Assets / Current Liabilities (Ideal: 2:1)
Gearing = Total Liabilities / Total Equity (Ideal: 0.5:1 to 1:1)
Gross Profit Ratio = Gross Profit / Sales × 100
Net Profit Ratio = Net Profit / Sales × 100
Return on Equity = Net Profit (after tax) / Total Equity × 100
Expense Ratio = Expenses / Sales × 100
AR Turnover = 365 / (Sales / Accounts Receivable) (Ideal: 30 days)
Break-Even = Fixed Costs / (Selling Price - Variable Cost per unit)
```
