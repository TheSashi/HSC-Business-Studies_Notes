## Key Formulas

```
Current Ratio = Current Assets / Current Liabilities
Gearing = Total Liabilities / Owner's Equity
Gross Profit Margin = (Gross Profit / Revenue) × 100
Net Profit Margin = (Net Profit / Revenue) × 100
Return on Equity = (Net Profit after tax / Owner's Equity) × 100
Expense Ratio = Total Expenses / Revenue
AR Turnover = 365 / (Revenue / Accounts Receivable)
Break-Even (units) = Fixed Costs / (Selling Price - Variable Cost per unit)
Working Capital = Current Assets - Current Liabilities
Capacity Utilisation = (Actual Output / Maximum Possible Output) × 100
```

## Key Exam Concepts

- **Interdependence** — all four functions are interconnected
- **Stakeholders** — employees, customers, shareholders, government, community
- **Globalisation** — impacts all four functions
- **Technology** — transforming all business functions
- **Ethics and corporate social responsibility** — increasing importance
- **Legal compliance** — Corporations Act, Fair Work Act, CCA, WHS Act

---

## HSC Exam Tips

1. **Always apply theory to a case study** — don't just describe, link to real business
2. **Use specific data** — ratios, figures, dates, percentages
3. **Show interdependence** — connect functions in extended responses
4. **Know your verbs** — identify (1-2 marks), explain (3-4 marks), evaluate (7-8 marks)
5. **Practice time management** — 3 hours for 100 marks
