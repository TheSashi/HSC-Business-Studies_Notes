# Extended Marketing Mix — People, Processes & Physical Evidence (The 7Ps)

> **HSC Business Studies — Marketing** | The extra 3 Ps that extend the traditional 4Ps marketing mix, particularly important for service-based businesses.

---

## 1. Why Extend the 4Ps?

The traditional marketing mix (Product, Price, Promotion, Place) focuses on goods. The extended marketing mix adds three more elements to address the unique challenges of **services**, which are:
- **Intangible** — can't be touched or tested before purchase
- **Inseparable** — produced and consumed simultaneously
- **Variable** — quality depends on who delivers it and when
- **Perishable** — can't be stored for later

The extra 3 Ps help businesses manage customer experience and service quality.

---

## 2. People

**People** refers to the quality of interaction between the customer and those within the business who deliver the service.

### Key Points
- Consumers base their perceptions and make judgements about a business based on **how employees treat them**
- All businesses should develop a **culture of customer focus** and put it into practice
- Staff training, attitude, and appearance directly impact customer satisfaction and loyalty

### Apple — People
- Strong focus on **sales and customer service training** in Apple stores
- Job titles such as **"Genius"** and **"Specialists"** to raise employee status and expertise
- In-store, online, and phone support for after-sales, repairs, complaints, and warranties

---

## 3. Processes

**Processes** refers to the flow of activities a business follows in the delivery of a service.

### Key Points
- Processes must be **efficient** to achieve customer satisfaction
- Includes ordering systems, payment methods, delivery procedures, complaint handling
- Streamlined processes reduce waiting times and improve customer experience

### Apple — Processes
- **Online ordering system** for convenience
- **Free iOS upgrades** for existing customers
- **Pre-ordering** available prior to new product launches (when purchasing directly from Apple)
- **Roaming payment system** in Apple stores so customers don't need to wait at a checkout

---

## 4. Physical Evidence

**Physical evidence** refers to the environment in which the service is delivered, plus tangible materials that support the brand image.

### Key Points
- Includes signage, brochures, letterheads, business logo, website, store design
- A business should provide **high quality physical evidence** to create an image of value and excellence
- The physical environment shapes customer perceptions before any interaction occurs

### Apple — Physical Evidence
- Apple stores are designed to reflect **innovation and quality**
- Encourage a **hands-on approach**, allowing customers to "test-drive" Apple products
- Elaborate building entrances for most stores, positioned in **prime city locations**
- Sleek packaging and professional website reinforce premium brand image

### Airline Example — Physical Evidence
Six elements an airline could use: **design, hygiene, uniform, decor, lighting, service**

---

## 5. Importance of the Extra 3 Ps — Apple Case Study

Apple benefits greatly from the extra 3 Ps because they help:
- **Strengthen its premium brand image** and improve customer experience
- Highly trained employees provide excellent customer service → **increases satisfaction and loyalty**
- Efficient processes (easy product setup, smooth online purchasing, regular software updates) make products **simple and convenient**
- Modern stores, sleek packaging, and professional website act as physical evidence of **quality and innovation**
- Helps maintain **competitive advantage** in a crowded market

---

## 6. HSC Exam Application

### Key Essay Structure for Extended Marketing Mix

When asked about target market and marketing strategies, link each of the extra 3 Ps:

**PEEL Structure — Processes Example:**
- **P**: Processes are influenced by the target market because a business designs processes to meet their needs
- **E**: Apple's processes include online ordering, pre-orders, roaming payment systems, free iOS upgrades
- **E**: These improve customer satisfaction by reducing waiting times and providing convenience — Apple customers expect speed and innovation
- **L**: Therefore, Apple's target market strongly influences its processes, helping maintain customer satisfaction, loyalty, and competitive advantage
