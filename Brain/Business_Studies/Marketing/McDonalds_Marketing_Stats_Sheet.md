# McDonald's Case Study Info

---

## Influences

- CCA 2010 enforced by ACCC — no misleading advertising, implied conditions, mandatory refunds
- Quick Service Restaurant Code — restricts children's advertising unless nutritional guidelines met
- Responsible Children's Marketing Initiative — self-regulation on kids marketing
- McDonald's Australia revenue (2024) — $25.9 billion
- McDonald's daily customers worldwide — 69 million
- Looser Change Menu items ($1–$3) introduced during GFC for price-sensitive consumers
- RBA cash rate decisions affect consumer disposable income and dining out spend
- MyMacca's loyalty members globally — 150 million+
- Happy Meals target families with children
- Menu adapted by country — McSpicy Paneer (India), Teriyaki Burger (Japan)
- Mass market positioning — accessible across all income levels

## Processes

- Australian fast food market share (2024) — 23.2%
- KFC Australian market share (2024) — 16.5%
- Total menu items — 100+
- Restaurants worldwide — 40,000+
- Countries operated in — 100+
- Loyalty members globally — 150 million+
- Primary research — customer satisfaction surveys, focus groups on new menu items
- Secondary research — industry reports, ABS data
- Research led to — salads, grilled chicken, fruit alternatives, self-order kiosks, delivery partnerships
- Core products (Big Mac, fries) in maturity stage
- New products (McPlant) in growth stage
- Menu item margins tracked through profitability analysis
- Financial forecasts compare projected costs (ingredients, labour, rent) against revenue

## Strategies

- Product mix — 100+ items (burgers, chicken, salads, McCafe, desserts, Happy Meals)
- Golden arches logo — one of the most recognised globally
- Packaging — consistent red/yellow branding across all restaurants
- Loss Leaders — Cheeseburgers, Frozen Cokes, Soft Serve on Looser Change Menu ($1–$3)
- Price Points — Value meals small ($5.90), medium ($7.90), large ($9.90)
- Prestige Pricing — McCafe coffees $4.50–$6.50
- Penetration Pricing — McDelivery launched at competitive prices
- Instagram followers in Australia — 2.5 million+
- Monopoly promotion increases sales — 15–20% during campaign
- Ronald McDonald House Charities (RMHC) — positive brand image
- MyMacca's App — personalised offers, mobile ordering, delivery tracking
- Loyalty members globally — 150 million+
- Restaurants worldwide — 40,000+
- Countries operated in — 100+
- Dine-in, drive-through (60%+ of Australian sales), McDelivery (Uber Eats), self-order kiosks
- Daily orders worldwide — 40 million+
- Hamburger University established 1961 — 275,000+ graduates
- 93% of McDonald's restaurants are franchise-operated
- Drive-through average service time — 3.5 minutes
- Daily customers worldwide — 69 million
- Bright lighting, clean tables, visible kitchen areas
- Digital menu boards, McCafe display cases, Playlands
