# Marketing — Section IV Essay Structures

> Scaffold templates for HSC Business Studies extended response questions (Section III/IV, 20 marks).

---

## 1. Assess/Evaluate/Explain the Importance of

**Verb**: Make a **judgement** based on criteria (does it help the business achieve _________?).

### Introduction
- Start with the **judgement sentence**
- State specifically what you are evaluating (use specific syllabus dot points)
- Example: "This essay will evaluate how Apple effectively uses their operational strategies of Supply Chain Management, Inventory Management and Outsourcing to respond to globalisation, ethical and legal influences."
- Make sure to include all case studies you will use in the essay

### Body Paragraph Structure

| Paragraph | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Paragraph 1** | Answer the question — judgement with brief overview of reasons. Start with judgement sentence. Include case study at every part of the paragraph. Define and describe key concept(s). |
| **Paragraph 2** | **Advantages** — prove they are advantages by linking to a topic-specific goal. Link each advantage as evidence of your judgement. Use stats where appropriate. |
| **Paragraph 3** | **Disadvantages** — prove they are disadvantages by linking to a topic-specific goal. |
| **Paragraph 4** | Re-state judgement, link to long-term business goals. Think about medium term/long term implications. |

### Conclusion
- Write 1-2 summary sentences (including case study) for each body paragraph

---

## 2. Analyse/Explain How

**Verb**: Show the relationship between ideas and draw out **implications** (long-term impacts).

### Introduction
- Start by linking the two ideas together (show cause and effect in the first sentence)
- State specifically what you are analysing (use specific syllabus dot points)
- Example: "This essay will analyse the impacts for Apple of globalisation, ethical and legal influences on their operational strategies of Supply Chain Management, Inventory Management and Outsourcing."
- Make sure to include all case studies

### Body Paragraph Structure

| Paragraph | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Paragraph 1** | **Cause/effect #1** — Link the two ideas together (cause and effect) for the case study. How (observable changes) is the business impacted? Why (link to topic-specific goal) is the business affected? Show clear cause and effect with case study evidence. |
| **Paragraph 2** | **Cause/effect #2** — Same structure. Show clear cause and effect. Link to cause #1. Use case study for evidence. |
| **Paragraph 3** | **Positive Implications (LONG TERM)** — Link to broader objective like strategic role. Link to business goals. What does this mean for the business? |
| **Paragraph 4** | **Negative Implications** — Point out an issue. |

> You do not have to give both positive and negative implications, but ensure they are detailed and linked to the case study.

### Conclusion
- Write 1-2 summary sentences (including case study) for each body paragraph

---

## 3. Key Exam Tips for Section IV

- Always use specific case study examples (Apple, McDonald's, BYD, etc.)
- Use business terminology from the syllabus
- Show cause-and-effect reasoning (how → therefore → which leads to)
- Link back to business goals (profit maximisation, growth, competitive advantage)
- Include case study at EVERY part of every paragraph
- Use the PEEL structure within each body paragraph
