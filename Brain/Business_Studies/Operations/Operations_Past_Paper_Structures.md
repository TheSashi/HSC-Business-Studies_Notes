# Operations HSC Exam Technique

> **HSC Business Studies — Operations Topic** | How to answer different verb types and past paper question structures.

---

## Verb Scaffolds

### Assess
**Meaning:** Make a judgement based on criteria.

**Structure:**
1. Judgement sentence — state your position and define the concept
2. How is it impacting on the operational process
3. Effect on the business
4. What does it mean for the business (implication)
5. Restate the judgement

**Example:** "Assess the impact of customised products on the operations process of a sports shoe store."
- Judgement: It has a large impact on the operational process...
- Define the process being affected
- Explain how customisation impacts inputs, technology, task design, layout
- Effect on the business
- Restate judgement

### Analyse
**Meaning:** Show the relationship and draw out implications.

**Structure:**
1. How does [concept] assist in achieving the strategic role of operations (cost leadership and product differentiation)
2. Define the concept
3. How does it impact on operations
4. Effect: link to performance objective
5. Implication: linking to cost leadership or differentiation

**Example:** "Analyse the use of outsourcing in achieving the strategic role of Operations Management."
- Define outsourcing
- How does outsourcing impact on operations
- Effect: link to performance objectives (cost, flexibility)
- Implication: linking to cost leadership

## Past HSC Short Answer Structures

### Q21(c): Assess the impact of customised products on operations

**Inputs:** Customers are a transformed resource
**Process:** Technology/Task Design/Layout affected

**Approach:**
- Customisation affects the type of inputs (customers become part of the process)
- Technology must support flexible production
- Task design shifts from standardised to skilled, varied work
- Layout may change from product to process layout

### Extended Response: Analyse outsourcing's role in strategic operations

**Approach:**
1. Define outsourcing
2. Link to cost leadership (reduces costs by using external specialists)
3. Link to product differentiation (access to expertise not available in-house)
4. Performance objectives affected: cost, quality, flexibility
5. Implications for long-term competitiveness

## Key Exam Tips

- **Read the verb carefully** — outline, explain, analyse, assess all require different depths
- **Link to the scenario** — always reference the specific business in the question
- **Use NESA terms** — interdependence, strategic role, value adding, competitive advantage
- **Integrate across topics** — show how operations connects to marketing, finance, HR
- **Provide implications** — what does this mean for the business's long-term success?
- For **assess** questions: always include a clear judgement at the start AND end
