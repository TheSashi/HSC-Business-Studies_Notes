## 1. Past HSC Short Answer Structure (2024)

### Question: Assess the impact of customised products on the operations process of the sports shoe store.

**Marking Approach (Assess = make a judgement based on criteria):**

**Inputs:** Customers are a transformed resource — their preferences directly shape the transformation process.

**Transformation:** Customisation requires flexible processes, skilled labour, varied materials — increases variety but may reduce volume.

**Outputs:** Higher customer satisfaction, premium pricing potential, but higher costs per unit.

**Judgement:** Customisation benefits the business through differentiation and customer loyalty, but increases operational complexity and costs. The impact is positive if the business can charge premium prices to offset increased costs.

## 2. Practice Questions by Topic

### Role of Operations
- Explain the difference between cost leadership and differentiation strategies
- Describe how operations contributes to profit maximisation
- Explain how operations is interdependent with marketing

### Processes
- Explain how the 4Vs influence the transformation process
- Describe the relationship between volume and variety in operations
- Explain how technology impacts the transformation process

### Strategies
- Compare quality control and quality assurance
- Explain the advantages and disadvantages of JIT inventory management
- Describe how supply chain management contributes to cost minimisation
- Evaluate the use of outsourcing as an operations strategy

### Influences
- Explain how globalisation affects the operations function
- Describe how technology has transformed operations management
- Assess the impact of environmental sustainability on operations strategies

## 3. Exam Scaffolds

### Explain Scaffold
- **A. Define** — What is it?
- **B. Cause/Relationship** — How does it impact operations?
- **C. Effects/Implications** — What does this lead to?
- **D. Link** — What does this mean for the business?

### Assess Scaffold
- **A. Introductory sentence** — State judgement
- **B. Criteria 1** — Evidence and analysis
- **C. Criteria 2** — Evidence and analysis
- **D. Conclusion** — Final judgement with justification

## 4. Apple Case Study — Operations Analysis

### Operations Strategy
| Strategy | Apple Implementation |
|----------|---------------------|
| **Outsourcing** | Foxconn manufactures iPhones; Apple focuses on design and marketing |
| **Quality Management** | Strict quality standards for suppliers; multiple supplier relationships |
| **Inventory Management** | Minimal inventory; demand-driven production |
| **Supply Chain** | Global web of suppliers; vertical integration (purchased Intel's 5G modem division) |
| **New Product Design** | Consumer-driven; market research informs design changes |

### Performance Objectives in Practice
- **Quality:** Premium products; "selling the best" — Tim Cook
- **Speed:** Rapid response to demand after new product launches
- **Dependability:** Consistent product quality; supplier dependency risks (Samsung OLED delays)
- **Flexibility:** iPhone 6 larger screens in response to competitor designs
- **Cost:** iPhone SE cheaper components; production moved to India; supplier switching

---

## 5. Key Exam Tips

1. **Always link theory to a case study** — Apple, McDonald's, Toyota
2. **Use specific examples** — products, strategies, figures
3. **Show interdependence** — connect operations to marketing, finance, HR
4. **Know your verbs** — outline (2), explain (3-4), assess (5-6), evaluate (7-8)
5. **Practice time management** — 20 marks = ~25 minutes for extended response

## 6. Key Formulas for Operations

```
Break-Even (units) = Fixed Costs / (Selling Price - Variable Cost per unit)
Capacity Utilisation = (Actual Output / Maximum Possible Output) × 100
Labour Productivity = Output / Number of Employees
```
