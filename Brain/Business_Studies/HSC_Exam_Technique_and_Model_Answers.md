# HSC Exam Technique & Model Answers

> Practical exam preparation guide with **model answers**, **markers' comments**, **scaffolds**, and **worked examples**. Use this alongside the topic theory notes: [[Finance_Management]] | [[Marketing]] | [[Operations]] | [[Human_Resources]]

## 1. Command Words — What Markers Want

| Verb | What to Do | Typical Marks |
|------|-----------|---------------|
| **Identify** | Name/state a concept | 1–2 |
| **Outline** | Give main features briefly | 2 |
| **Describe** | Give characteristics and features | 2–3 |
| **Explain** | Show how/why, cause and effect | 3–4 |
| **Analyse** | Break down + examine relationships | 4–5 |
| **Discuss** | Present arguments for and against | 5–6 |
| **Assess** | Make a judgement based on criteria | 5–6 |
| **Evaluate** | Make judgement with supporting arguments | 7–8 |
| **Justify** | Support a decision with evidence | 6–8 |

## 2. Response Scaffolds

### 2.1 PEEL Paragraph Structure

- **P** — Point (state your argument/thesis)
- **E** — Explain (define and elaborate)
- **E** — Evidence (case study example, statistics, specific details)
- **L** — Link (connect back to the question)

### 2.2 "Explain" Scaffold

```
A. Define — What is it?
B. How/Why — Cause and effect (use relational verbs)
C. Effect — What does this lead to?
D. Link — Therefore... (link to business objective/goal)
```

### 2.3 "Assess" Scaffold

```
Judgement sentence: "It has a large impact on..." + define the concept
↓
How is it impacting on the process/strategy?
↓
Effect on the business
↓
What does it mean for the business? (implication)
↓
Restate the judgement
```

### 2.4 "Analyse" Scaffold

```
Define the concept
↓
How does it impact on operations/marketing/finance?
↓
Effect: link to the performance objective
↓
What does it mean for the business? (implication)
```

### 2.5 Ratio Comparison Structure (Finance)

1. **State the ratio** — "The current ratio has worsened from 1.43:1 in 2022 to 1.16:1 in 2023"
2. **Define what it measures** — "The current ratio measures liquidity, which is the ability to pay current liabilities with current assets"
3. **Identify similarity** — "One similarity is that inventory remained around $52 million"
4. **Identify difference** — "One difference is the increase in short-term borrowing of $2 million"
5. **Link to cause** — "Therefore this shows why the current ratio has worsened"

### 2.6 Financial Strategy Recommendation Structure

1. **Recommend** — "It is recommended that [business] use [strategy]"
2. **Define** — "[Strategy] is..."
3. **Reason 1** — How it improves performance
4. **Reason 2** — Additional benefit
5. **Link** — Restate recommendation with justification

## 3. Model Answers — Finance (McDonald's Case Study)

### 3.1 Liquidity — Current Ratio

| | 2022 | 2023 |
|---|---|---|
| Current Ratio | 1.43:1 | 1.16:1 |

> The current ratio has worsened from 1.43:1 in 2022 to 1.16:1 in 2023. The current ratio measures liquidity which is the ability of the business to pay its current liabilities with their current assets. One similarity between 2022 and 2023 is the value of McDonald's inventory as they are both around $52 million. One difference between 2022 and 2023 is the huge increase of short-term borrowing of $2 million. Therefore this shows why the current ratio has worsened.

### 3.2 Profitability — Net Profit Ratio

| | 2022 | 2023 |
|---|---|---|
| Net Profit Ratio | 27% | 33% |
| Yum Brands comparison | 19% | 23% |

**Calculation:** NP = NP/Sales × 100 → 6177.4/23182.6 × 100 = 27%

> **Assess introductory sentence:** The profitability of McDonald's has improved from 2022 to 2023. This demonstrates that the profitability strategies are effective as the Net Profit ratio has improved from 27% to 33%.
>
> **Cause and How:** McDonald's has implemented a new marketing strategy in 2023 which has led to an increase in sales from $23,182 million to $25,493 million. McDonald's started a new promotional campaign where they gave away movie figures from Marvel with the large-size meals. This incentivised more customers to buy their products and recommend them to their friends and family.
>
> **Effect:** This led to more customers buying their products and increasing sales. As they were able to maintain their costs this led to an improvement in profitability. Therefore showing that their profitability strategies have been effective.
>
> **Linking sentence:** As profitability has increased this means that McDonald's has money to be able to expand their business and achieve their growth objective with their profitability objective. Therefore McDonald's profitability strategies have been effective.

## 4. Model Answers — Past HSC Finance (2025)

### Question (a): Outline the expense ratio for 2024 (2 marks)

> Expense ratio in 2024 = 21,000/700,000 = 30%
>
> This means for every $1 of sales, $0.30 are expenses. Additionally, comparing to industry average of 20% in 2024, this travel agency's expense ratio is worse because it is higher than the industry average.

**✅ Markers' Comment:** Student achieved 2/2 — showed all working for the calculation AND stated what it means for the business while comparing to the industry average. Related it to the scenario.

### Question (b): Explain how comparative ratio analysis improves financial outcome (4 marks)

> Comparative ratio analysis can improve this travel agency's financial outcomes. Through comparative ratio analysis the travel agency's expense ratio in 2024 (30%) to the industry average in 2024 (20%), because the travel agency's expense ratio is higher than the industry average, it indicates that expenses per sales have increased, meaning less net profit. As a result, financial managers can use this data and revise or change their recent financial strategies, leading to a better financial outcome for the travel agency because they'll be able to use the comparative ratio analysis to understand whether their financial strategies have been achieving objectives like efficiency.

**✅ Markers' Comment:** Student achieved 4/4 because they:
- Linked it to the scenario
- Showed clear cause and effect relationships (using relational verbs)
- Showed implications (limited but present)

### Question (c): Explain how TWO global market influences could affect solvency (4 marks)

> **Interest rates** affect the solvency of this travel agency because it can cause an increase in current liabilities. When global interest rates increase, the travel agency will have to pay greater amounts of interest payments. This increases the total liabilities they owe, which leads to an increase in debt-to-equity ratio (gearing) resulting in the travel agency becoming less solvent.
>
> Furthermore, **economic outlook** is another global market influence that can impact the solvency of this travel agency. This is because when the economic outlook is negative, this travel agency will be less likely to take on debt and invest because it is less profitable. As a result, the debt-to-equity ratio may decrease or even stay the same resulting in a maintained gearing, hence allowing this travel agency to maintain its solvency.

**✅ Markers' Comment:** Student achieved 4/4 — linked to scenario, showed cause and effect with relational verbs, provided implications.

### Key Takeaways from Markers

- ✅ **Always link to the scenario** — don't just state generic theory
- ✅ **Use relational verbs** — "because", "as a result", "therefore", "this leads to"
- ✅ **Show cause AND effect** — not just one
- ✅ **Provide implications** — what does this mean for the business?
- ✅ **Show all working** for calculations

## 6. Model Answers — Operations Example Paragraphs

### 6.1 Strategies → Performance Objectives

> **Global sourcing** is important for a business such as Apple in achieving their performance objective of **cost**. Global sourcing is when inputs such as raw materials are sourced globally to obtain the best cost input while still maintaining quality. Apple sources their inputs from a number of different suppliers globally such as from China, South East Asia, Europe. They have sourced these goods to ensure that they are achieving **economies of scale** as they can buy in bulk from these suppliers at a cheaper cost. In addition they are ensuring that they source from suppliers where they have maintained quality so even though they are reducing costs and achieving **cost leadership**, they are still producing a quality product. Therefore due to the reduced production costs where they are achieving their performance objective of cost, Apple is able to allocate the additional funds to other operational areas such as research and development. This shows how global sourcing is important for Apple to utilise to achieve the performance objective of cost.

### 6.2 Evaluate Strategies in Response to Influences

> Apple's global sourcing strategy allows it to respond effectively to **cost-based competition**. Global sourcing is where Apple sources its inputs globally to find the best quality inputs for the lowest cost to respond to the influence of cost-based competition. Cost-based competition occurs in markets like the smartphone market where there are many competitors and businesses such as Apple need to be able to minimise costs to be able to compete. Apple does this by sourcing their inputs from many different countries such as China, America, South East Asia, European countries. They do this so that they can achieve cost leadership by buying quality inputs for a lower cost which assists Apple in maintaining a competitive advantage to ensure that they can respond to the influence of cost-based competition. This means that Apple can reduce its cost to reduce their prices to become more competitive in the market. Therefore global sourcing is an effective strategy to respond to the influence of cost-based competition.

### 6.3 Cross-Topic Integration (Finance + Operations)

> One strategy that XYZ Retail can use to overcome their issue with having high inventory levels is to use the **JIT inventory management strategy** to control their current asset of inventory. XYZ Retail has high inventory levels which is increasing costs for the business as they have to pay to store the inventory and there is a risk that the inventory will become obsolete and therefore not convert into sales revenue. This will impact on the **profitability** of XYZ Retail as they may not generate as much sales revenue and their expenses will increase, leading to a decrease in their net profit. By using JIT inventory strategy XYZ Retail will reduce their storage costs which will assist them in minimising expenses to assist with improving profitability. In addition it will reduce the risk that the inventory will not be able to convert into sales due to obsolescence, although there could be a risk that they do not have enough stock on hand to meet customer demands which could potentially reduce sales revenue. By reducing inventory costs XYZ Retail is able to reduce expenses and maximise their profit which can be reinvested into the business leading to business growth.

> **💡 Tip:** Top students integrate strategies from other topics (e.g., operations strategies linked to finance objectives). Always link back to the financial impact.

## 7. Financial Ratios — Quick Reference Card

### 7.1 All Ratios at a Glance

| # | Ratio | Formula | Objective | Ideal | Linked Strategies |
|---|-------|---------|-----------|-------|-------------------|
| 1 | **Current Ratio** | Current Assets / Current Liabilities | Liquidity | 2:1 | Control over CA & CL; Leasing; Sale and Leaseback |
| 2 | **Gearing Ratio** | Total Liabilities / Total Equity | Solvency | 0.5:1 to 1:1 | Choose best source of financing |
| 3 | **Gross Profit Ratio** | Gross Profit / Sales × 100 | Profitability | Compare to periods & industry | Revenue control (marketing mix); Cost controls (bulk buying, global sourcing, outsourcing) |
| 4 | **Net Profit Ratio** | Net Profit / Sales × 100 | Profitability | Compare to periods & industry | Same as gross profit |
| 5 | **Return on Owners Equity** | Net Profit / Total Equity | Profitability | Compare to periods & industry | Same as gross profit |
| 6 | **Expense Ratio** | Total Expenses / Sales | Efficiency | Lower is better | Cost controls |
| 7 | **Accounts Receivable Turnover** | Step 1: Sales / AR → Step 2: 365 / answer | Efficiency | 30 days | Factoring; Discounts for early payment; Credit policies |

### 7.2 Must-Memorise Equations

**Cash Flow Statement:**
> Opening Cash Balance + Cash Receipts (inflow) − Cash Payments (outflow) = Closing Cash Balance

**Income Statement:**
> Sales
> − Cost of Goods Sold (Opening Stock + Purchases − Closing Stock)
> = Gross Profit
> − Expenses
> = Net Profit

**Balance Sheet:**
> Owners Equity = Assets (Current + Non-Current) − Liabilities (Current + Non-Current)

### 7.3 Inventory Valuation — LIFO vs FIFO

| Situation | To Minimise Profit (reduce tax) | To Maximise Profit (attract investors) |
|-----------|--------------------------------|---------------------------------------|
| **Cost of inventory increasing** | LIFO | FIFO |
| **Cost of inventory decreasing** | FIFO | LIFO |

> ⚠️ **Exam trap:** If the question asks about **inventory management strategy** → discuss Holding Stock or JIT. If it asks about **inventory valuation** → discuss LIFO and FIFO.

## 8. Common Mistakes to Avoid

| ❌ Mistake | ✅ Fix |
|-----------|--------|
| Stating theory without linking to scenario | Always reference the specific business from the question |
| Using "this shows" without explaining WHY | Use relational verbs: "because", "as a result", "therefore" |
| Only showing cause without effect | Always complete the chain: Cause → Effect → Implication |
| Calculating ratios without interpretation | Always state what the ratio MEANS for the business |
| Writing generic responses | Include specific numbers, dates, campaign names |
| Forgetting to make a judgement (Assess/Evaluate) | Start AND end with your judgement |
| Mixing up inventory management and valuation | Management = JIT/Holding Stock; Valuation = LIFO/FIFO |

## 9. HSC Question Verb Toolkit

### "Outline" (2 marks)
```
State the concept + brief explanation
Example: "The expense ratio measures... In 2024 it was 30%, meaning..."
```

### "Explain" (4 marks)
```
Define → Cause → Effect → Link to business goal
```

### "Discuss" (5–6 marks)
```
Argument FOR → Evidence → Argument AGAINST → Evidence → Balanced JUDGEMENT
```

### "Evaluate/Assess" (7–8 marks)
```
Criteria → For (with evidence) → Against (with evidence) → Weighed JUDGEMENT → Recommendation
```

## 10. Detailed Verb Scaffolds — Sentence by Sentence

### 10.1 DESCRIBE

**What it means:** Give a clear account of what something is. Identify key features.

**Structure:**

**Sentence 1 — Define**
[KEY TERM] refers to [formal definition from syllabus].

**Sentence 2 — Features/Characteristics**
It involves [feature 1], [feature 2], and [feature 3].

**Sentence 3 — Example**
For example, McDonald's uses [specific example] which demonstrates [how it works].

**Sentence 4 — Link**
This is important for McDonald's because [reason].

**Tip:** Keep it short. Describe is usually 1-2 marks. Don't overthink it.

---

### 10.2 EXPLAIN (Cause and Effect)

**What it means:** Show WHY something happens and WHAT effect it has. Two part answer.

**Structure:**

**Sentence 1 — Define**
[KEY TERM] refers to [formal definition].

**Sentence 2 — Cause (Why)**
This impacts McDonald's because [cause/reason].

**Sentence 3 — Effect (What happens)**
As a result, [specific effect on the business].

**Sentence 4 — Link**
Therefore, [key term] is important for McDonald's [marketing/operations/etc] because [link to business goal].

**Example:**
Market segmentation refers to dividing a market into distinct groups of buyers. This impacts McDonald's because different consumer groups have different needs and preferences. As a result, McDonald's develops targeted marketing strategies for families (Happy Meals), young adults (McCafe), and budget conscious consumers (Loose Change Menu). Therefore, market segmentation is important for McDonald's as it allows the business to meet diverse customer needs and increase market share.

**Tip:** Always answer BOTH parts. Why does it happen? What is the effect?

---

### 10.3 EXPLAIN HOW

**What it means:** Show the relationship between two things. How does A affect B?

**Structure:**

**Sentence 1 — Link the two ideas**
[THING A] affects [THING B] because [reason].

**Sentence 2 — How (observable change)**
This is demonstrated through [specific example of how A changes B].

**Sentence 3 — Why it matters**
This matters because [link to business objective].

**Sentence 4 — Implications**
As a result, McDonald's [outcome/impact].

**Example:**
Target market impacts on people strategies because McDonald's designs its workforce approach to meet the expectations of its core consumers. This is demonstrated through the Mates Make It Maccas campaign which targets young Australian workers aged 15-24, offering flexible hours and career development. This matters because McDonald's target market values convenience and speed, requiring staff who can deliver fast service. As a result, McDonald's invests in comprehensive training programs to ensure employees meet customer expectations.

**Tip:** Always show the CONNECTION between the two things. Don't just describe each separately.

---

### 10.4 ANALYSE

**What it means:** Show the relationship between ideas and draw out implications (long term impacts).

**Structure:**

**Sentence 1 — Define and link**
[THING A] is related to [THING B] because [reason].

**Sentence 2 — Cause/effect #1**
[THING A] affects [THING B] through [specific example]. This means [implication].

**Sentence 3 — Cause/effect #2**
Furthermore, [another example of relationship]. As a result, [implication].

**Sentence 4 — Positive implications**
In the long term, this [positive impact on business].

**Sentence 5 — Negative implications**
However, [potential negative impact or limitation].

**Example:**
Situational analysis directly impacts marketing strategies at McDonald's. SWOT analysis reveals that McDonald's strength in brand recognition and scale allows it to invest heavily in digital transformation, with 65% of orders now placed through digital channels. Furthermore, identifying threats from health conscious consumer trends has led McDonald's to develop healthier menu options and transparent nutritional labelling. In the long term, this strategic awareness positions McDonald's to maintain its 23.2% market share. However, failing to adequately address weaknesses such as negative health perceptions could erode brand loyalty over time.

**Tip:** Analyse requires showing HOW things are connected AND what the implications are. Don't just list facts.

---

### 10.5 EVALUATE / ASSESS

**What it means:** Make a judgement. How effective or important is something? Weigh up advantages and disadvantages.

**Structure:**

**Introduction:**
[KEY TERM] is [judgement sentence, e.g. highly important/effective]. This essay will evaluate [topic] by examining [point 1], [point 2], and [point 3].

**Body 1 — Advantage #1**
One advantage is [advantage]. For example, [specific evidence with stats]. This demonstrates [why it is effective].

**Body 2 — Advantage #2**
Additionally, [second advantage]. [Evidence and reasoning]. This contributes to [business objective].

**Body 3 — Disadvantage #1**
However, there are disadvantages. [Disadvantage]. For example, [evidence]. This may [negative impact].

**Body 4 — Disadvantage #2 / Limitation**
Furthermore, [second disadvantage]. [Evidence]. Despite this, [counter argument or mitigation].

**Conclusion:**
In conclusion, [restate judgement]. Although [disadvantages], the advantages [outweigh/are significant because]. McDonald's [strategy] contributes to [business goals such as market share, revenue, customer loyalty].

**Tip:** You MUST make a clear judgement. Don't sit on the fence. Say whether it is effective or not effective, important or not important.

---

### 10.6 DISCUSS

**What it means:** Present arguments for AND against, then make a balanced judgement.

**Structure:**

**Introduction:**
[STATE THE ISSUE]. This essay will discuss arguments for and against [topic].

**Body 1 — Argument FOR**
One argument for [topic] is [reason]. For example, [evidence]. This suggests [positive implication].

**Body 2 — Argument FOR (continued)**
Furthermore, [second reason FOR]. [Evidence]. This demonstrates [positive outcome].

**Body 3 — Argument AGAINST**
However, one argument against [topic] is [reason]. For example, [evidence]. This suggests [negative implication].

**Body 4 — Argument AGAINST (continued)**
Additionally, [second reason AGAINST]. [Evidence]. This raises concerns about [negative outcome].

**Conclusion:**
In conclusion, while [arguments for], [arguments against] cannot be ignored. Overall, [balanced judgement with reasoning].

**Tip:** Give EQUAL weight to both sides. Don't spend 3 paragraphs on FOR and 1 on AGAINST.

---

### 10.7 JUSTIFY

**What it means:** Provide reasons and evidence to support a position. Prove that something is the right choice.

**Structure:**

**Sentence 1 — State position**
[THING] is justified because [primary reason].

**Sentence 2 — Evidence #1**
Firstly, [reason with evidence]. For example, [specific McDonald's example with stats].

**Sentence 3 — Evidence #2**
Secondly, [reason with evidence]. This is demonstrated by [example].

**Sentence 4 — Address counter argument**
While [potential counter argument], this is outweighed by [reason].

**Sentence 5 — Link**
Therefore, [restate position] as it contributes to [business goal].

**Tip:** Justify is like a one sided evaluate. You are proving your position is correct.
