# Financial Institutions, Government Influence & Global Markets

> **HSC Business Studies — Finance** | Financial institutions, ASX, government regulation (RBA, ASIC, tax), and global market influences.

---

## 1. Financial Institutions

### Investment Banks (e.g., Macquarie Bank)
- Trade in money, securities and financial futures
- Arrange **long-term finance** for company expansion
- Advise clients on **foreign exchange cover**
- Advise on **mergers** (e.g., Westpac and St George) and **takeovers**
- Operate **unit trusts** (for wealthy investors to minimise tax)
- Arrange **overseas finance** — cheaper interest rates from overseas banks

### Finance and Life Insurance Companies
- Non-bank financial intermediaries specialising in smaller commercial finance
- Provide unsecured and secured loans to businesses and individuals
- Premiums from insurance policies used to lend to businesses; income from interest payments

### Superannuation Funds
- Provide funds to corporate sector through investment of superannuation contributions

### Unit Trusts (Mutual Funds)
- Take funds from many small investors and invest in specific financial assets

---

## 2. Australian Securities Exchange (ASX)

The ASX functions as a **market operator**, **clearing house** and **payment system facilitator**.

| Role | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Primary Market** | New shares sold; company raises new capital directly from investors |
| **Secondary Market** | Pre-owned securities traded between investors; company receives no money |

> **Key distinction:** Primary market = company gets money. Secondary market = exchange between investors, company gets nothing.

### ASX Functions
- Oversees compliance with operating rules
- Promotes **corporate governance** standards among listed companies
- Offers products and shares

## 3. Influence of Government

### Monetary Policy (RBA)
- RBA assists federal government to achieve **low inflation** and **economic growth**
- Securities and loans are sold/bought to pressure interest rates and alter the economic cycle
- **Impact on business:** Interest rate increases → higher debt servicing costs for businesses

### ASIC (Australian Securities and Investment Commission)
- Enforces and administers the **Corporations Act**
- Protects consumers in investments, insurance, superannuation and banking
- Aims to reduce **fraud and unfair practices** in financial markets
- Ensures companies adhere to law; makes company information public

### Company Taxation
- Companies pay tax on profits **before** dividends are distributed to shareholders
- **Impact:** Reduces net profit available for distribution; makes debt more attractive than equity (interest payments are tax-deductible)

## 4. Global Market Influences

Financial risks in global markets are greater than domestic but necessary for business strategy.

| Influence | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Interest Rates** | Comparison of domestic vs international rates — borrowing overseas may be cheaper |
| **Availability of Funds** | Ease of accessing funds on international financial markets |
| **Global Economic Outlook** | Projected changes to economic growth worldwide |

### Current Interest Rates (approximate)
| Country | Rate |
|---------|------|
| Australia | 4.35% |
| USA | 4.25–4.5% |
| UK | 4.5% |
| Japan | 0.5% |
| China | 3.10% |
| India | 6.25% |

### Impact of Negative Global Outlook
- Lenders become reluctant to lend (fear of non-payment)
- Harder for businesses to find loans
- Higher interest rates on available loans
- Reduced ability to access global funds

## 5. Comparison Ratio Analysis

Comparing ratios to:
- **Previous periods** — track improvement/decline over time
- **Industry averages** — benchmark against competitors

> This allows businesses to evaluate whether strategies are working and make informed adjustments.
