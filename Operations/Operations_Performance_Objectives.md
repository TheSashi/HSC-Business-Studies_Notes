# Operations Performance Objectives

> **HSC Business Studies — Operations Topic** | The 6 performance objectives and their application.

---

## Definition

Performance objectives are the goals that a business's operations function is trying to achieve. They guide operational decision-making and measure success.

---

## The 6 Performance Objectives

### 1. Quality

Two dimensions:
- **Quality of Design** — arises from understanding consumers and their preferences
- **Quality of Conformance** — how well the product meets the standard of a prescribed design with certain specifications; measures how consistently products achieve compliance with desired specifications

### 2. Speed

The time it takes for production and operations processes to respond to changes in the market. Aims to satisfy customer demands as quickly as possible.

Goals for speed:
- Reduced wait times
- Shorter lead times
- Faster processing times

### 3. Dependability

How consistent and reliable a business's products are. Customers expect products to perform as promised, every time.

### 4. Flexibility

How quickly operation processes can adjust to changes in the market. Time and flexibility are related — the quicker the processing time, the greater the likelihood that processes can be adjusted quickly.

### 5. Customisation

Creation of individualised products to meet specific customer needs. Based on the principle of **mass customisation** — a process that allows a standard, mass-produced item to be modified to a specific customer requirement.

### 6. Cost

Minimisation of expenses such that operation processes are conducted as cheaply as possible. All KPIs will be based around minimisation of cost.

## Apple — Performance Objectives Case Study

| Objective | Apple Application |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Quality | Premium materials, rigorous testing, high design standards |
| Speed | Fast product launches, efficient supply chain, quick customer service |
| Dependability | Consistent product quality across all devices, reliable software updates |
| Flexibility | Adapts product line to market trends (e.g., AirPods, Apple Watch) |
| Customisation | Personalised engraving, build-to-order Mac configurations |
| Cost | Economies of scale, efficient manufacturing, premium pricing offsets costs |

## Key Exam Points

- **Identify** which performance objectives are most relevant to specific business scenarios
- **Analyse** how performance objectives support the strategic role of operations (cost leadership vs differentiation)
- **Explain** the trade-offs between objectives (e.g., quality vs cost, speed vs customisation)
- Link performance objectives to **KPIs** and **operations strategy**
