# HSC Operations — Extended Response Questions & Model Answers (2015–2019)

## How to Write Extended Response Questions

### Structure

| Section | Requirements |
|---|---|
| **Introduction** | Main point answering the question, specific syllabus reference, introduce case study/ies |
| **Body Paragraphs** | Integrate case study into each point. Minimum 2 paragraphs (3 recommended). Separate paragraphs for each main point |
| **Conclusion** | 1–2 sentences summarising each body paragraph |

## 2015: Evaluate the Importance of Operation Strategies in Achieving Competitive Advantage

### Key Term: Evaluate
> Make a judgement based on criteria — here the criteria is **achieving competitive advantage**

### Recommended Strategies (Choose 3)
- [[Operations/Operations_Strategies|Supply Chain Management]] — choose one: Logistics, Global Sourcing, or E-Commerce
- Technology
- Outsourcing
- Inventory Management
- Quality Improvement — choose one: Quality Control, Quality Assurance, or Quality Improvement
- New Product Design and Development
- [[Operations/Operations_Influences|Global Factors]] — choose one: Global Sourcing, Economies of Scale, Scanning and Learning, or R&D

> **NOT recommended:** Performance Objectives or Overcoming Resistance to Change

### Sample Introduction
> *It is very important for Apple to choose the most effective operational strategies to assist them in achieving competitive advantage. As Apple produces new technology such as smartphones, tablets and computers, they are in a very competitive market therefore it is extremely important that they choose the most appropriate operational strategies to ensure that they are achieving cost leadership and product differentiation to maintain their competitive advantage. This essay will demonstrate the importance of Apple using Global Sourcing, Quality Control and Research and Development to maintain their competitive advantage.*

### Sample Body Paragraph — Global Sourcing

**Structure:**
1. **Judgement sentence** — *It is extremely important for Apple to use Global Sourcing to maintain their competitive advantage*
2. **Definition** — Global sourcing is the sourcing of raw materials from businesses overseas
3. **Cause** — Apple sources raw materials from over 30 different countries from Asia, Europe and America
4. **How** — Complex supply chain with contracts for competitive prices while maintaining high quality standards
5. **Effect** — Logistics challenges but cost savings, achieving cost leadership while maintaining quality
6. **Link to competitive advantage** — Apple maintains strong market position through global sourcing
7. **Business goal** — Minimising costs and increasing market share assists in achieving profit maximisation

## 2016: Evaluate the Response of Operations Management to External Influences

### Key Term: Evaluate
> Make a judgement based on criteria — here the criteria is **responding to external influences**

### Strategy-Influence Links
| Strategy | Link to Influence |
|---|---|
| SCM (Logistics/Global Sourcing/E-Commerce) | Technology, Quality Expectations, Cost-based Competition |
| Technology | Technology, Quality Expectations, Cost-based Competition |
| Outsourcing | Cost-based Competition, CSR |
| Quality Management | Quality Expectations |
| Global Factors | Globalisation |

### Sample Introduction
> *Operations management plays a crucial role in enabling a business to respond effectively to external influences in order to remain competitive. External influences such as technological change, quality expectations, and globalisation can significantly impact how a business operates. This essay will evaluate the response of Apple's operations management to external influences by examining the operations strategies of Technology, Quality Control, and Global Sourcing.*

### Model Body Paragraphs

#### 1. Technology → Technological Change
- **Definition:** Rapid advancements in digital systems, automation, and innovation
- **Apple's response:** Heavily integrates advanced technology (automation, robotics, AI) into manufacturing and product design
- **Effect:** High quality products, reduced human error, lower production costs, cost leadership
- **Evaluation:** Highly effective — allows Apple to remain competitive in cost-based and innovation-driven market

#### 2. Quality Control → Quality Expectations
- **Definition:** Consumers demanding reliable, durable, high-performing products
- **Apple's response:** Strict quality control at every production stage — testing components, inspecting finished products, monitoring supplier standards
- **Effect:** Minimises defective products, maintains brand reputation
- **Trade-off:** Increases production costs but justified through pricing and brand loyalty
- **Evaluation:** Effective — meets customer expectations, differentiates from competitors

#### 3. Global Sourcing → Globalisation
- **Definition:** Businesses operating on an international scale with access to overseas markets, suppliers, and labour
- **Apple's response:** Sources from China, Taiwan, South Korea — lower costs, access to specialised suppliers
- **Challenges:** Supply chain disruptions, ethical concerns
- **Mitigation:** Long-term supplier contracts, strict compliance standards
- **Evaluation:** Effectively manages risks while remaining competitive globally

## 2017: Analyse the Effect of Globalisation on Operations Management

### Key Term: Analyse
> Show the relationship between and draw out implications

### Sample Introduction
> *Performance objectives and operations strategies are closely linked, as operations strategies are implemented to achieve specific performance objectives such as cost, quality, speed, dependability and flexibility. This essay will analyse the relationship between performance objectives and operations strategies by examining Apple as a case study.*

### Model Body Paragraphs

#### 1. Technology → Cost & Speed
- **Definition:** Use of advanced machinery, automation and digital systems in production
- **Apple:** Highly automated manufacturing, advanced software systems
- **Relationship:** Increases production speed, reduces reliance on labour, produces large volumes efficiently
- **Implication:** Faster production + lower labour costs = improved speed and cost efficiency

#### 2. Quality Control → Quality & Cost
- **Definition:** Inspecting products during and after production to ensure standards
- **Apple:** Strict testing of components and finished products
- **Relationship:** Meets customer expectations for premium quality, strengthens brand reputation
- **Long-term benefit:** Reduces returns, repairs, and warranty claims — lowering long-term costs

#### 3. Global Sourcing → Cost & Dependability
- **Definition:** Obtaining raw materials and components from overseas suppliers
- **Apple:** Sources from China, Taiwan, South Korea — specialised suppliers at lower costs
- **Relationship:** Reduces production costs while ensuring consistent supply
- **Dependability:** Long-term contracts ensure on-time delivery and maintained production schedules

## Performance Objectives (5 Key Areas)

| Objective | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Cost** | Minimising production and operational expenses |
| **Quality** | Meeting or exceeding customer expectations |
| **Speed** | Reducing production and delivery time |
| **Dependability** | Consistent, reliable operations and delivery |
| **Flexibility** | Ability to adapt to changes in demand or market |

---

## Key Exam Technique Tips

1. **Always define key terms** — show the examiner you understand the concepts
2. **Use specific case study data** — numbers, dates, facts (e.g., "785 suppliers in 31 countries")
3. **Link cause → effect → implication** — don't just describe, show the relationship
4. **Make judgements** for "evaluate" questions — use criteria from the question
5. **Draw out implications** for "analyse" questions — what does this mean for the business?
6. **3 body paragraphs minimum** for extended responses
7. **Conclusion should summarise**, not introduce new information
