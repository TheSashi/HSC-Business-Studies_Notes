# Inventory Valuation Methods (LIFO vs FIFO)

> **HSC Business Studies — Operations Topic** | How businesses value inventory and the financial implications.

---

## Important Distinction

- If the question asks about **Inventory Management strategy** → only use **Holding Stock** or **Just in Time (JIT)**
- If the question asks about a strategy to **value inventory** or an **Inventory Valuation method** → talk about **LIFO** and **FIFO**

---

## LIFO (Last In, Last Out)

The most recently purchased stock is sold first.

## FIFO (First In, First Out)

The oldest stock is sold first.

---

## When to Use Each Method

| Scenario | To Minimise Profit (reduce tax) | To Maximise Profit (attract investors) |
|----------|--------------------------------|---------------------------------------|
| Cost of inventory is **increasing** | LIFO | FIFO |
| Cost of inventory is **decreasing** | FIFO | LIFO |

**Logic:**
- If costs are rising and you use LIFO, the higher recent costs are recorded as COGS → lower profit → less tax
- If costs are rising and you use FIFO, the lower older costs are recorded as COGS → higher profit → better for investors

---

## Past HSC Questions

**Q1:** A business is experiencing increasing costs for its stock over time. It is seeking to maximise its profit for the current financial period. Which strategy should it adopt to value its inventory?
- a) First In First Out
- b) Just in case
- c) Just in time
- d) Last in First Out

**Answer: A (FIFO)** — increasing costs + maximise profit = FIFO (records lower older costs as COGS → higher profit)

**Q2:** A business is experiencing decreasing costs for its stock over time. It would like to minimise the amount of tax they pay therefore wish to minimise profit. Which strategy should it adopt to value its inventory?
- a) First in First Out
- b) First in Last Out
- c) Last in Last Out
- d) Just in Time

**Answer: A (FIFO)** — decreasing costs + minimise profit = FIFO (records higher older costs as COGS → lower profit → less tax)

---

## Key Exam Points

- **Distinguish** between LIFO and FIFO valuation methods
- **Apply** the correct method based on the business's financial objectives
- **Explain** how inventory valuation affects reported profit and tax obligations
- Remember: LIFO/FIFO are **valuation** methods, not **management** strategies
