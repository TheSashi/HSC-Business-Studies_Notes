# Operations Influences — Cost, Legal, Environmental

> **HSC Business Studies — Operations Topic** | How cost-based competition, government policies, legal regulations, and environmental sustainability influence operations.

---

## 1. Cost-Based Competition

Derived from determining break-even point and applying strategies to create cost advantages over competitors. Reducing cost is the main way to maximise profitability.

**Ways to reduce costs:**
1. Eliminate waste
2. Produce standardised products for larger markets
3. Produce high volume output
4. Use automated production systems
5. Achieve economies of scale
6. Bulk buy inputs

---

## 2. Government Policies

All businesses operate in a political-legal environment. Political decisions impact business operations.

**Key policies include:**
- Environmental policies (carbon reduction targets)
- Workplace Health and Safety (WH&S) standards
- Employment relations
- Taxation
- Trade and industry-based policies
- Labelling requirements (country of origin for production and inputs)

---

## 3. Legal Regulations (Compliance)

The range of laws with which a business must comply = **compliance**. The expenses associated with meeting these requirements = **compliance costs**.

**Key legislation:**
1. **WH&S** — Work and Health Safety Act 2012 (Commonwealth)
2. **Training and Development** — use and application of technology, appropriate work methods
3. **Fair Work and Anti-Discrimination** — Racial Discrimination Act 1975, Anti-Discrimination Act 1977 (NSW), Fair Work Act 2009 (Cwlth)
4. **Environmental Protection** — Environment Protection and Biodiversity Conservation Act 1999 (Cwlth)
5. **Fair Trading** — Competition and Consumer Act 2010 (Cwlth)
6. **Food Safety** — Food Safety Act 2003 (NSW)

---

## 4. Environmental Sustainability

Growing pressure on businesses to operate sustainably. Key considerations:
- Reducing carbon footprint
- Sustainable sourcing of materials
- Waste reduction and recycling
- Energy efficiency
- Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR)
- Ethical responsibility to stakeholders

---

## 5. Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR)

Businesses have responsibilities beyond profit-making:
- Ethical treatment of workers
- Environmental stewardship
- Community engagement
- Transparent reporting
- Balancing stakeholder interests (shareholders, employees, community, environment)

---

## Apple — Influences Case Study

- **Cost:** Achieves economies of scale through high-volume manufacturing; uses global supply chain to minimise costs
- **Legal:** Complies with WH&S in retail stores; follows environmental regulations for product disposal
- **Environmental:** Carbon neutral commitments; recycled materials in products; trade-in programs
- **CSR:** Supplier responsibility reports; privacy as a fundamental right; education initiatives

---

## Key Exam Points

- **Explain** how cost-based competition influences operations decisions
- **Analyse** the impact of legal regulations on operations processes
- **Assess** how environmental sustainability influences operations strategy
- Distinguish between **compliance** and **voluntary** environmental strategies
