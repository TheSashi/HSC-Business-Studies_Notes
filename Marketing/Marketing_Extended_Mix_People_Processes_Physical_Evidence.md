# Marketing — People, Processes, Physical Evidence

> The traditional 4Ps focus on goods. The extended 3Ps address **services**, which are intangible, inseparable, variable, and perishable.

---

## 1. People

**Definition**: The quality of interaction between the customer and those within the business who will deliver the service.

- Consumers base their perceptions and make judgements about a business based on how the employees treat them
- All businesses should develop a **culture of customer focus** to put it into practice
- Customer focus increases customer base, reputation, sales and company revenue

### Apple Case Study — People
- Strong focus on **sales and customer service training** in Apple stores
- Job titles such as "**Genius**" and "**Specialists**" to raise employee status
- In-store, online and phone support for after-sales, repairs, complaints and warranties

### Why Customer Focus Matters
- A customer makes their perception and judgments based on how the employees/services treat them
- Focusing on customer service is vital for future expansion options and increasing brand image
- Helps target a wider customer base

---

## 2. Processes

**Definition**: The flow of activities that a business will follow in the delivery of a service.

- Processes must be **efficient** to achieve customer satisfaction
- Smooth, convenient processes improve customer experience and loyalty

### Apple Case Study — Processes
- **Online ordering system**
- **Free upgrade of iOS** for existing customers
- **Pre-ordering available** prior to new product launches (when purchasing directly from Apple)
- **Roaming payment system** in Apple stores so customers don't need to wait at a checkout

---

## 3. Physical Evidence

**Definition**: The environment in which the service will be delivered, including materials such as signage, brochures, letterheads, business logo and website.

- A business should provide **high quality physical evidence** to create an image of value and excellence
- Physical evidence acts as tangible proof of brand quality and innovation

### Apple Case Study — Physical Evidence
- Apple stores are designed to reflect the importance of **innovation and quality**
- Encourage **hands-on approach**, allowing customers to "test-drive" Apple products
- **Elaborate building entrances** for most stores, positioned in prime city locations
- Sleek packaging, professional website

### Six Elements of Physical Evidence (Airline Example)
1. Design
2. Hygiene
3. Uniform
4. Decor
5. Lighting
6. Service

---

## 4. Activity Solutions

### Q1: Why should all businesses develop a culture of customer focus?
Businesses should develop a strong culture of customer focus to increase their customer base and reputation which will in the future increase their sales and company revenue. Customer focus is a vital element of a business as a customer makes their perception and judgments based on how the employees/services treat them. Therefore focusing on customer service is vital for a business for future expansion options and increasing their brand image targeting a wider customer base.

### Q2: Why is it important to extend the marketing mix in the current business environment?
The extended marketing mix (3Ps) is important because services have unique characteristics (intangible, inseparable, variable, perishable) that the traditional 4Ps do not fully address. People, processes and physical evidence help businesses differentiate their service offering, build customer trust, and create competitive advantage in an increasingly service-oriented economy.

### Q3: Explain the importance to Apple of the extra 3Ps
Apple benefits greatly from the extra 3Ps of the marketing mix because they help strengthen its premium brand image and improve customer experience. Apple's highly trained employees provide excellent customer service, which increases customer satisfaction and loyalty. Its efficient processes, such as easy product setup, smooth online purchasing, and regular software updates, make products simple and convenient to use. In addition, Apple's modern stores, sleek packaging, and professional website act as physical evidence of quality and innovation, helping the company maintain its strong reputation and competitive advantage.
