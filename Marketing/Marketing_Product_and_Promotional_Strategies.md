# Product Strategies & Promotional Mix

> **HSC Business Studies — Marketing** | Total product concept, branding, packaging, product life cycle, and promotional mix strategies.

---

## 1. Total Product Concept

Most products are a combination of **tangible and intangible** components. Customers buy both.

A product is a **collection of satisfactions** including:
- The package
- The brand name
- The warranty
- The after-sale service

---

## 2. Product Branding

A **brand** = a name, term, symbol, design or any combination that identifies a specific product and distinguishes it from competitors.

### Branding Strategies

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Manufacturer's/National Brand** | Owned by manufacturer; high appeal; recognised nationally |
| **Private/Household Brand** | Owned by retailer or wholesaler |
| **Generic Brands** | No brand name; usually supermarket products |

### Why Brands Matter
- Instant recognition of business and reputation
- Showcases quality of products/services
- Builds customer loyalty and trust
- Must be linked to **reputation of the business**

---

## 3. Packaging

**Packaging** = development of a container and graphic design for a product.

### Packaging as Marketing Strategy
- **Recognition** — colours, branding, logo make product easily identifiable
- **Implied conditions** — packaging must show true size of product
- **Differentiation** — unique packaging distinguishes from competitors
- **Information** — provides product details, ingredients, instructions

---

## 4. Product Life Cycle

| Stage | Characteristics | Marketing Focus |
|-------|----------------|-----------------|
| **Introduction** | New product, low sales, high costs | Educate customers, heavy promotion |
| **Growth** | Rapidly increasing sales, competition enters | Build brand loyalty, expand distribution |
| **Maturity** | Peak sales, intense competition | Differentiate, maintain market share |
| **Decline** | Falling sales, reduced profits | Harvest or discontinue |

---

## 5. Promotional Mix

The **promotional mix** = combination of promotional strategies used to communicate with target markets.

| Strategy | Description | Advantages | Disadvantages |
|----------|-------------|------------|---------------|
| **Advertising** | Paid, non-personal communication through mass media | Wide reach, builds brand awareness | Expensive, one-way communication |
| **Personal Selling** | Face-to-face communication with potential customers | Customised message, immediate feedback | Expensive per contact, limited reach |
| **Sales Promotion** | Short-term incentives to encourage purchase | Immediate impact, stimulates trial | Temporary effect, may cheapen brand |
| **Public Relations** | Building good relationships with public and media | Credibility, low cost | Hard to control, slow to build |
| **Direct Marketing** | Direct communication with targeted individuals | Personalised, measurable | Can be intrusive, expensive |

### Answering an "Assess" Question on Promotional Mix

**"Assess why a promotional mix of promotion strategies is important in the marketing of goods and services"**

**Scaffold:**
1. **Define** promotional mix
2. **Identify** strategies in the mix (advertising, personal selling, sales promotion, PR, direct marketing)
3. **Explain** how each strategy contributes to marketing objectives
4. **Assess** — make a judgement about the overall importance
5. **Link** to case study

---

## 6. Marketing Mix — The 4Ps

| P | Description | Key Considerations |
|---|-------------|-------------------|
| **Product** | Goods/services offered | Quality, features, branding, packaging, warranty |
| **Price** | Amount charged | Cost-plus, competitive, penetration, skimming |
| **Promotion** | Communication strategies | Advertising, PR, sales promotion, personal selling |
| **Place** | Distribution channels | Retail, online, wholesale, intensive/selective/exclusive |

---

## 7. Monitoring and Controlling the Marketing Plan

- Monitor **sales data** against marketing objectives
- Use **KPIs** (Key Performance Indicators) to measure success
- Adjust marketing mix based on performance data
- **Customer feedback** is essential for continuous improvement
