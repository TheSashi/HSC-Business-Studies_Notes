# Product Strategies

> **HSC Business Studies — Marketing Topic** | Total product concept, branding, packaging, and Apple case study.

---

## Total Product Concept — Tangible and Intangible

Most products are a combination of tangible and intangible components. When customers purchase products, they buy both tangible and intangible benefits.

A **product** is a collection of satisfactions which include:
- The package
- The brand name
- The warranty
- The after-sale service

Marketing managers prefer the term "total product concept" because customers don't just buy the physical item — they buy the entire experience including branding, service, and support.

---

## Product Branding

A **brand** is a name, term, symbol, design or any combination of these that identifies a specific product and distinguishes it from competitors.

**Brand symbol/logo** — graphic representation that identifies a business or product.

### Branding Strategies

1. **Manufacturer's (national) brand** — owned by the manufacturer; high customer appeal because recognised across the country
2. **Private (household) brand** — owned by a retailer or wholesaler
3. **Generic brands** — products with no brand name; normally used for supermarket products
4. Branding must be linked to the **reputation** of the business

### Why Businesses Invest in Branding

- Sociocultural and psychological factors — brand name/logo is a way to recognise a business
- Instantly communicates reputation and reliability
- Showcases quality of products/services offered
- Considerations: target market, colour appeal, professionalism, implied conditions

---

## Packaging

Development of a container and graphic design for a product.

Key considerations:
- How easily is the packaging recognised by customers (colours, branding, logo)
- Does it meet implied conditions (ensuring packaging shows true size of product — e.g., check packs)

---

## Apple — Product Case Study

- **Total product concept:** iPhone = hardware + iOS ecosystem + AppleCare warranty + Genius Bar support
- **Branding:** Apple logo (bitten apple) is one of the most recognised globally; linked to innovation and premium quality
- **Packaging:** Minimalist white/silver design; high-quality materials that reinforce luxury image
- **Differentiation:** Products positioned as high-quality, innovative, premium — commands higher prices

- **Explain** why marketing managers use "total product concept" rather than just "product"
- **Analyse** how branding strategies influence consumer perceptions
- **Assess** the role of packaging in supporting brand positioning
- Link product strategy to **target market** and **competitive advantage**
