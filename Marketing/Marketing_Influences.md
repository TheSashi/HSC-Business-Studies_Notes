# Marketing Influences — Consumer Law, Ethics & Consumer Behaviour

> **HSC Business Studies — Marketing** | Legal influences (CCA, implied conditions, warranties), ethical influences, and factors influencing customer choice.

---

## 1. Consumer Law

Governments (federal and state) have introduced laws to protect consumer rights and clarify business responsibilities.

### Competition and Consumer Act 2010 (CCA)

**Two major purposes:**
1. Protect consumers against **undesirable practices** (misrepresenting products, misleading advertising)
2. Regulate trade practices that **restrict competition**

**Enforced by:** ACCC (Australian Competition and Consumer Commission) and ASIC

---

## 2. Deceptive and Misleading Conduct

The following are **illegal** under the CCA:

| Practice | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Fine Print** | Important conditions in small print, difficult to read |
| **Before and After Ads** | Misleading comparisons showing dramatic changes |
| **Tests and Surveys** | Unsubstantiated claims |
| **Country of Origin** | Misleading about where product was made |
| **Packaging** | Size/shape gives misleading impression of contents |
| **Special Offers** | Advertising "limited time" offers that are continuously available |

### Common Misleading Techniques

| Technique | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Bait and Switch** | Advertise few products at reduced prices; when they run out, direct customers to higher-priced items |
| **Dishonest Advertising** | Using deceptive words or claiming product has qualities it doesn't; price reductions and free gifts must be genuine |

---

## 3. Price Discrimination

- Setting different prices for a product in separate markets
- Difference possible due to: geographic spread, product differentiation within market
- **CCA prohibits** price discrimination if it could substantially reduce competition
- Also applies to discounts, credits, rebates, services, payment arrangements

---

## 4. Implied Conditions

**Implied conditions** = unspoken and unwritten terms of a contract that exist regardless of whether they're written in.

| Condition | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Fitness for Purpose** | Product must be suitable for its intended purpose |
| **Quality** | Illegal to suggest product has characteristics it doesn't have |

---

## 5. Warranties

A **warranty** = promise by the business to repair/replace faulty products.
- Ensures customers the business has **confidence in quality**
- Can be an **aggressive marketing tool** if it includes superior options to competitors

---

## 6. Refunds and Exchanges

A business is **required by law** to offer a refund if:
- Products are faulty
- Products don't match description
- Products don't do what they're supposed to

---

## 7. Ethical Influences on Marketing

### Truth and Accuracy in Advertising

**Unethical marketing practices include:**

| Practice | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Untruths due to Concealed Facts** | Hiding important information from consumers |
| **Puffery** | Exaggerated claims that can't be objectively measured |
| **Stereotyping** | Portraying groups in simplified, often offensive ways |
| **Exploitation of Fears** | Using fear to manipulate purchasing decisions |
| **Subliminal Messages** | Hidden messages designed to influence subconsciously |

---

## 8. Factors Influencing Customer Choice

### Psychological Factors
- **Motivation** — internal needs that drive behaviour
- **Perception** — how consumers interpret information
- **Learning** — changes in behaviour from experience
- **Attitudes** — learned tendencies to respond consistently

### Sociocultural Factors
- **Culture** — shared values, beliefs, customs
- **Social Class** — income, education, occupation
- **Reference Groups** — people who influence attitudes/values
- **Family** — primary influence on consumer behaviour

### Economic Factors
- **Disposable Income** — money available after tax
- **Discretionary Income** — money available after necessities
- **Consumer Credit** — ability to borrow
- **Economic Conditions** — inflation, unemployment, interest rates

---

## 9. HSC Exam Tips

- **Consumer law** questions: Always cite the **CCA 2010** and specific provisions
- **Ethics** questions: Distinguish between what is **legal** and what is **ethical** — they're not the same
- **Consumer behaviour** questions: Link factors to specific **examples** and **case studies**
