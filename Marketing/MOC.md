## Key Exam Concepts

- **Interdependence** — marketing connects to operations (demand forecasts), finance (budgets/revenue), HR (trained staff)
- **Customer orientation** — all actions aimed at satisfying customer needs
- **Marketing approach** — find what customer wants, then satisfy that need
- **CCA 2010** — protects consumers against misleading conduct and anti-competitive behaviour
