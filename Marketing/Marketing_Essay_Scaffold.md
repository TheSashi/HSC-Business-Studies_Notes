# Section IV Essay Scaffold — Marketing

Use this to plan your 800-1000 word essay. Fill in the [BRACKETS].

## Introduction (~100 words)

> [KEY TERM] refers to [formal definition]. McDonald's has effectively implemented [STRATEGY/APPROACH] to [ACHIEVE OBJECTIVE]. This essay will evaluate [TOPIC] by examining [POINT 1], [POINT 2], and [POINT 3], demonstrating [THESIS JUDGEMENT].

## Body 1 — Context / Background (~150 words)

> McDonald's, the world's largest QSR with [STAT], has [STRATEGY/APPROACH] to address [INFLUENCE/ISSUE]. [Explain what they did with specific detail]. This has [EFFECT/OUTCOME] because [REASON].

## Body 2 — Strategy/Process 1 (~200 words)

> One key [strategy/process] McDonald's employs is [NAME]. [Explain what it is]. For example, [SPECIFIC EXAMPLE FROM McDONALD'S]. This has been effective because [EVALUATION with reasoning]. Consequently, [IMPACT on business objective].

## Body 3 — Strategy/Process 2 (~200 words)

> Furthermore, [SECOND STRATEGY] has enabled McDonald's to [OUTCOME]. [Explain with detail]. This is particularly significant because [EVALUATION]. As evidenced by [STAT/EVIDENCE], this approach has [IMPACT].

## Body 4 — Evaluation / Challenges (~200 words)

> Despite these successes, McDonald's faces [CHALLENGE/LIMITATION]. [Explain the issue]. However, McDonald's has responded by [COUNTER-STRATEGY]. While [LIMITATION], the overall impact remains [POSITIVE/NEGATIVE] because [REASONED JUDGEMENT].

## Conclusion (~100 words)

> In conclusion, McDonald's [APPROACH/STRATEGY] has been largely [EFFECTIVE/INEFFECTIVE] in [CONTEXT]. Through [STRATEGY 1] and [STRATEGY 2], the company has [OUTCOME]. Despite [CHALLENGE], the evidence demonstrates that [FINAL JUDGEMENT]. McDonald's [APPROACH] positions the business to [FUTURE OUTLOOK].

## Exam Day Tips

- Read the question carefully — identify the VERB (evaluate, discuss, analyse, explain) and the TOPIC
- Use RELATIONAL verbs: evaluate, assess, analyse, justify — not just describe
- Include AT LEAST 2 specific McDonald's statistics per body paragraph
- Use formal academic language — no slang, no contractions, no first person
- Each body paragraph: Topic Sentence → Explain → Evidence → Evaluate → Link
- Time management: 10 mins planning, 45 mins writing, 5 mins checking
- Reference the info sheet — adapt to the question, don't copy word for word
