# Promotion Strategies

> **HSC Business Studies — Marketing Topic** | Promotion mix, advertising, PR, and communication process.

---

## Definition

**Promotion** describes the methods used by a business to inform, persuade and remind a target market about its products.

---

## Elements of the Promotion Mix

### 1. Advertising

The six main advertising media:
1. Mass marketing (TV, radio, newspapers)
2. Direct marketing catalogues
3. Telemarketing
4. E-marketing (email campaigns)
5. Social media advertising (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube)
6. Billboards (outdoor advertising)

### 2. Personal Selling and Relationship Marketing

- **Personal selling** — activities of a sales consultant directed to a customer to make a sale; involves the human aspect of promotion
- **Relationship marketing** — development of long-term, cost-effective, strong relationships with individual customers
- Example: Flybuys, Everyday Rewards cards, Apple Genius Bar

### 3. Sales Promotion

Direct inducement to customers to sell more products. Techniques include:
1. Coupons
2. Premiums — gift offered in return for using their products
3. Refunds
4. Samples
5. Point-of-purchase displays

### 4. Publicity and Public Relations

- **Publicity** — any free news about a business's products
- **Public Relations (PR)** — activities aimed at creating and maintaining favourable relations between business and customers

PR activities include:
1. Promoting a positive image
2. Effective communication of messages
3. Issues monitoring
4. Crisis management

---

## The Communication Process

Marketing managers must communicate clearly, efficiently and succinctly to target markets using a variety of channels.

**Noise** — any interference or distraction that affects any or all stages in the promotional communication process.

---

## Opinion Leaders and Word of Mouth

- **Opinion leaders** — a person who influences others because their opinions are respected; often sought out for advice
- Businesses use celebrities, influencers, experts as opinion leaders
- **Word of mouth** — people influence each other during conversations
- Word of mouth can be more powerful than other promotion techniques because it comes from trusted personal sources

---

## Apple — Promotion Case Study

- Heavy use of social media and e-marketing (Apple Events livestreamed globally)
- Minimal traditional advertising — relies on brand reputation and word of mouth
- PR through product launches (keynote presentations)
- Personal selling through Apple Store "Geniuses" and "Specialists"
- Relationship marketing through Apple ecosystem (iCloud, Apple ID, Apple Music)

---

## Key Exam Points

- **Outline** the elements of the promotion mix
- **Distinguish** between publicity and public relations
- **Explain** how target market influences promotion strategy choices
- **Analyse** the effectiveness of different promotion techniques for different products
