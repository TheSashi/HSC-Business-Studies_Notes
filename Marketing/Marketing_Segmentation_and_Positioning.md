# Market Segmentation, Differentiation & Product Positioning

> **HSC Business Studies — Marketing** | Segmentation variables, differentiation strategies, and product positioning techniques.

---

## 1. Market Segmentation

**Market segmentation** = dividing the total market into segments, then selecting one segment as a target market. Often a business will implement a specific marketing mix for each target market.

A **segmentation variable** = characteristics used by marketing managers to divide a total market.

### Ways to Segment Markets

| Method | Description | Examples |
|--------|-------------|---------|
| **Demographic** | Age, gender, income, cultural background, family size | Youth market, seniors, high-income earners |
| **Geographic** | Geographic locations | Urban vs rural, climate zones, suburbs |
| **Psychographic** | Personality, motives, opinions, socioeconomic groups, lifestyles | Health-conscious, luxury seekers, environmentally aware |
| **Behavioural** | Customer's relationship to the product — knowledge, attitude, use, benefits sought | Brand loyalty, usage rate, benefits sought |

---

## 2. Product/Service Differentiation

**Differentiation** = making similar products appear different from and/or better than competitors.

### Points of Differentiation

| Point | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Customer Service** | Training programs, after-sales support, personalised experience |
| **Environment Concerns** | Sustainable practices, eco-friendly packaging |
| **Convenience** | Location, delivery options, ease of use |
| **Social and Ethical Issues** | Fair trade, ethical sourcing, community involvement |

---

## 3. Product Positioning

**Product positioning** = creating an image or identity for a product compared with competing products. Done through how potential buyers from the target market **perceive** the product.

### How Positioning Works
- Some brand names evoke certain images (e.g., No Frills = budget, Rolex = luxury)
- The business decides on the desired image and uses other elements of the marketing mix to shape and maintain it

### Apple Case Study — Positioning
- Apple positions itself as a **premium, innovative** brand
- Uses sleek design, premium pricing, and exclusive retail experience
- Differentiates through **ecosystem integration** (iPhone, Mac, Apple Watch, AirPods all work together)

---

## 4. Link Between Segmentation, Targeting and Positioning

```
Total Market → Segmentation (divide) → Target Market (select) → Positioning (create image)
```

1. **Segment** the market using demographic, geographic, psychographic, or behavioural variables
2. **Select** the most attractive segment as the target market
3. **Position** the product to appeal to that target market's needs and perceptions

---

## 5. HSC Exam Tips

- When asked about **segmentation**, always provide **specific examples** for each type
- **Differentiation** questions require linking the point of differentiation to **competitive advantage**
- **Positioning** questions should explain how the image **influences marketing strategies**
